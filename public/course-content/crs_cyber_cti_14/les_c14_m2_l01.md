| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Passive collection | uses information already published or recorded by third parties, without interacting with the subject's systems beyond normal public access: published reports, passive DNS databases, certificate transparency logs, registration data (RDAP), archived web pages, public advisories. |
| Active collection | interacts directly with the subject: connecting to its servers, probing services, downloading payloads, engaging with people. |

Active steps can alert an adversary, affect systems you do not own, and raise legal and ethical questions; in this course they are out of scope. The single live lookup (Module 3) is ordinary public access to example.com - one DNS query, one TLS handshake, one RDAP request.

Analyst safety and organisational exposure also matter: research from a managed, monitored environment approved by your organisation, not from a personal device, and follow your organisation's OSINT policy on accounts and tools. This course does not teach covert identities; where an organisation needs them, they require legal approval and a documented policy.
