## About this module

Everything in this course ends in a product someone reads and acts on. This module teaches how to build that product: an intelligence brief that answers requirements, leads with the bottom line, states judgments with calibrated likelihood and separate confidence, cites graded sources, admits gaps, and recommends specific defensive actions with owners. You will assemble the MONSOON LANTERN brief from the work of Modules 1-7 and check it with brief_builder.py, which rejects a deliberately poor draft for six reasons and accepts the model brief.

## Why it matters

Consumers judge intelligence teams by their products. A brief that buries the conclusion, overstates attribution or gives no actions wastes the analysis behind it. Good advisories show the alternative: CISA's July 2023 advisory on the Microsoft cloud intrusion (AA23-193A) told defenders which log events to look for and what baseline to compare against (case study CS1) [R2].

## Where it is used

Flash notes to SOC and IR leads, campaign reports to security management, strategic assessments for executives and boards, ISAC contributions and customer advisories.

## Learning objectives

1. Choose the right product type, length and format for each audience.
2. Structure a brief with BLUF, key judgments, evidence, gaps, recommendations and sources.
3. Cite sources with grades and link them to the collection record.
4. Write defensive recommendations that are specific, owned, tied to requirements and measurable.
5. Apply quality control, including automated checks and peer review.
6. Disseminate with correct handling and collect feedback.
