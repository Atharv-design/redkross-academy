1. Handling marking (TLP) and title, date, review-by date.
2. Bottom line up front (BLUF): two or three sentences answering the requirement.
3. Key judgments: each with a likelihood term, a separate confidence statement and source citations (Module 1).
4. Evidence and analysis: what was observed, by whom, graded; what is assumed; alternative hypotheses (Module 4).
5. Recommended actions: specific, owned, tied to requirements, with time frames.
6. Intelligence gaps and planned collection.
7. Sources: identifiers, grades and dates.
8. Annexes as needed: indicators (or a pointer to the STIX collection), ATT&CK layer, timeline.

Write for the reader's decision. If the CISO reads only the BLUF, the BLUF must be enough to act on.
