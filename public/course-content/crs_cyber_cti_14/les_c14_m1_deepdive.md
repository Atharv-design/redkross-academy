**Interpretation:**

- The tree makes gaps visible to the consumer: PIR-3 needs Legal and the DPO to define what they need before anyone can collect.
- Draft v1 line 3 states as fact a claim from the weakest source in the set; line 4 sounds professional but breaks the separation rule.
- In v2 the attribution judgment became "Attribution is unlikely to be resolved with current collection ... Confidence: low." - a useful, honest statement in place of a false certainty.

**Limitations:** the linter checks form, not truth; a well-formed judgment can still be wrong. Keyword matching in requirements_view.py is crude by design (L03).
