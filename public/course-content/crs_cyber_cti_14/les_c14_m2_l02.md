The Berkeley Protocol describes preparation and an investigation process [R1]:

```text
Preparation: digital threat and risk assessment; digital landscape
assessment; an online investigation plan; resilience and self-care;
data policies and tools.
Investigation process: online inquiries; preliminary assessment;
collection; preservation; verification; investigative analysis.
Reporting: written, oral or visual reporting of findings.
```

Applied to CTI, a short plan before collecting answers: which requirement (PIR/SIR) is served; which sources and tools are allowed; what personal data might be encountered and how it will be handled; how material will be preserved; how findings will be verified; and when to stop. The plan is what makes the inquiry reviewable later.

Verification deserves its own step: check dates (is the page current?), provenance (who first published it?), consistency with other evidence, and technical checks (does the domain resolve as claimed in passive DNS records?). A screenshot of a forum post is a claim, not a fact.
