Proportionate actions for each finding:

- block (proxy, DNS resolver) dedicated campaign domains and addresses, with an expiry (Module 5);
- monitor, not block, shared infrastructure; block the specific domain instead;
- watch for new registrations and certificates matching the pattern;
- report abuse to the registrar or hosting provider and, for brand impersonation, pursue takedown through the proper channel;
- share the cluster with partners as structured intelligence (Module 6) with the evidence for each link.

Never take active measures against third-party infrastructure; disruption is for providers and authorities.
