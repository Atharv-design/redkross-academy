## Revision

### Summary

Choose the product for the audience: flash notes for operational urgency, campaign reports for management and engineers, strategic assessments for executives, STIX for tools. A brief has a TLP marking, BLUF, key judgments with likelihood, separate confidence and citations, evidence and alternatives, owned and measurable recommendations, gaps, sources and a review-by date. Cite graded sources and link them to the collection log (ICD 203's first tradecraft standard). Recommendations must be specific, owned, tied to PIRs and proportionate. brief_builder.py rejected the poor draft for six reasons (TLP, judgment form, unknown source, weak-only support, unowned action, no review date) and passed the model brief; peer review and challenge cover substance. Disseminate by marking, version updates and collect feedback. AA23-193A showed the value of telling readers exactly which evidence to look for.

### Important concepts

BLUF; calibrated judgments; graded citations; owned actions; quality control.

### Key technical takeaways

automate form checks; render confidence separately; version every update.

### Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| BLUF | Bottom line up front. |
| Flash note | Short, urgent intelligence product. |
| Review-by | Date by which a product must be re-assessed. |

### Common mistakes

burying conclusions; unsourced judgments; unowned actions; silent edits.

### Revision questions

​1. List the brief sections. 2. Name the six checks. 3. Write a recommendation that meets all criteria.
