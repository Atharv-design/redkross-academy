**Good recommendations are:**

- specific: "block the 10 active campaign indicators at the proxy and DNS resolver for 14-30 days (per type), then review" rather than "block the threat";
- owned: a named role (SOC lead, IR lead, detection engineering);
- tied to a requirement (PIR-2) so the consumer sees why;
- time-bound and measurable (expiry dates, acceptance tests);
- proportionate: the Module 3 and 5 lessons - no blocks on shared infrastructure - apply.

**From this course:** block and watch validated indicators (Module 5); investigate the confirmed contact (u017, 10.40.3.57) with evidence preservation (Course 09); close the most valuable visibility gaps (scheduled-task telemetry, Module Load, Network Traffic Content - Module 7); monitor lookalike domains and certificates (Module 3); share with the ISAC under the right markings (Module 6).
