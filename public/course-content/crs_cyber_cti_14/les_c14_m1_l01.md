| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Data | raw observations: a log line, a domain registration |
| Information | data organised and in context: "this domain was registered yesterday and resolves to 198.51.100.77" |
| Intelligence | information analysed and evaluated against a question: "the domain is very likely part of a phishing campaign aimed at our customers; block it now" |

Intelligence is produced for a consumer and a decision. Four common levels, each with different consumers, time horizons and products:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| strategic | board, CISO, risk committee; months to years; trends, actor intent, sector risk; prose assessments |
| operational | security leadership, IR managers; weeks; campaigns, likely next steps; briefs and campaign reports |
| tactical | SOC, detection engineers; days; techniques and behaviours; detection content, hunt hypotheses |
| technical | SOC tooling; hours; indicators (addresses, domains, hashes); machine-readable feeds |

A single event feeds several levels: the MONSOON LANTERN case (used in every module) produces indicators for the proxy (technical), technique coverage work (tactical), a flash brief to the CISO (operational) and, possibly, a sector-risk paragraph for the board (strategic).
