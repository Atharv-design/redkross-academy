Intelligence is valuable when it changes decisions. Measures that matter:

- timeliness: was it delivered before the decision?
- relevance: did it answer a current requirement?
- actionability: what was done because of it (blocks, patches, hunts, budget decisions)?
- accuracy over time: were judgments borne out (track and review)?
- consumer satisfaction: short, regular feedback, not annual surveys.

Count of indicators shared is not a value measure; 81 alerts of which 8 are real (Module 5) is negative value.
