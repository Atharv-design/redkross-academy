DNS records most used in CTI:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A / AAAA | addresses a name resolves to |
| CNAME | an alias to another name (common with CDNs and, in SUNBURST, used to hand victims to command-and-control servers) |
| NS | the authoritative name servers - often shared by thousands of domains at cheap providers |
| MX, TXT | mail servers; verification and policy records |

Registration data (now usually through RDAP, the successor to WHOIS queries) gives the registrar, creation and expiry dates and status codes; registrant contact details are usually redacted for privacy. Creation date is the most useful field: Nilgiri's lure domains were created between 20 and 28 September 2026, days before the first lure on 2-3 October.

Lookalike domains imitate a brand: typosquats (nilgrimutual), combosquats that add words (portal-nilgiri-claims, nilgiri-policy-renewal), different top-level domains, and internationalised characters that look like Latin letters. Brand-protection monitoring watches new registrations and certificate logs (L04) for such names.
