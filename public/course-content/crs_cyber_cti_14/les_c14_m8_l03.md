ICD 203's first analytic tradecraft standard asks products to properly describe the quality and credibility of underlying sources, data and methodologies [R1]. In practice:

- every judgment cites the reports it rests on ([RPT-004]);
- the sources section gives each report's source, grade (Module 2) and date;
- the collection log (Module 2) links cited material to hashes and collection time;
- uncorroborated weak sources are never the only support for a judgment; if they are mentioned, they are mentioned with their grade.

The model brief cites RPT-003 (E5) only inside a judgment that says attribution is not possible, alongside better-graded reports - showing the weak claim was considered and set aside.
