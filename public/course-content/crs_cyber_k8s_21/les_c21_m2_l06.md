An SBOM answers "what is in it". Provenance answers "how, where and from what was it built": the builder, the build definition (Dockerfile, build arguments), the source revision and the resolved inputs (base image digest). Together they let a consumer verify that the image they run came from the reviewed source through the approved pipeline. SLSA expresses this as build levels, from "provenance exists" to provenance generated and signed by a hardened, hosted builder (Module 7). Even at the lowest level, recording the base image digest and source commit in the image (OCI annotations such as org.opencontainers.image.revision) speeds up incident response.
