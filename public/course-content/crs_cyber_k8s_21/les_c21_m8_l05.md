**Two audiences:**

- Technical: control-by-control results with evidence lines, changes made, remaining failures with owners and dates, how to reproduce.
- Management: a one-page summary - score before and after, the three most important risks reduced, the residual risks with business meaning, decisions needed (budget, accepted risk, deadlines).

A score such as 10/10 is a statement about ten chosen tests, not a statement that the cluster is secure. Always state what the catalogue does not cover: for example node-level runtime detection (Module 5 L06), signature verification at admission (Module 7 L06), kubelet configuration, add-on vulnerabilities and the security of the pipelines that deploy to the cluster. Residual risk is a decision someone must own.
