An attestation is a signed statement about an artefact, in the in-toto format: a subject (the image digest), a predicate type (what kind of statement) and a predicate (the content). Common predicates are SLSA provenance, SBOMs (CycloneDX, SPDX), vulnerability scan results and test results. The lab attached the CycloneDX SBOM from Module 2 with "cosign attest --type cyclonedx" and verified it; the decoded statement showed predicateType [https://cyclonedx.org/bom](https://cyclonedx.org/bom), the subject digest 07dcdcda..., and the 39 components of the SBOM. Policies can then require, for example, "an SBOM attestation signed by the release pipeline exists for this digest" or "a scan attestation newer than seven days with no fixable CRITICAL findings".

**Note:** attestations accumulate. Each lab run added another SBOM attestation to the same digest (three were returned after three runs). Policies should pick the newest valid attestation from a trusted signer rather than assume there is one.
