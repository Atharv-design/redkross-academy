Assets are what you must protect: customer personal data (names, addresses, phone numbers), credentials and tokens, payment data, business data such as shipment status, code and signing keys, and availability of the service. For each asset, state the properties it needs:

- confidentiality (who may read it);
- integrity (who may change it, and how changes are detected);
- availability (how much downtime is tolerable);
- plus authenticity, non-repudiation and privacy.

Personal data of people in India brings obligations under the DPDP Act, 2023 and its Rules as they commence (Course 01 Module 7): purpose limitation, security safeguards and breach intimation. Record which data stores hold personal data, because that drives logging, retention and encryption decisions.
