**Useful metrics:**

- Mean time to remediate vulnerabilities, by severity, compared with the service-level objectives.
- Percentage of repositories with SAST, dependency and secret scanning enabled.
- Number of security findings introduced per release (trend).
- Escape rate: vulnerabilities found in production that a gate should have caught.
- Waivers open and expired.
- Training completion and secure-code review coverage.

Maturity models such as OWASP SAMM can guide a multi-year roadmap. Review the programme after every significant incident: which lifecycle activity should have caught it?
