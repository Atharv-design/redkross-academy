Most traffic is TLS-encrypted, so payload signatures see less every year.

**What remains visible:** DNS (unless it is encrypted with DoH or DoT), the server name in the TLS ClientHello (unless Encrypted Client Hello is used), certificate details for TLS 1.2, flow sizes and timing. Organisations respond with egress through inspecting proxies for managed devices, with endpoint telemetry, and by controlling which resolvers are allowed. Packet capture involves personal data and communications content. In India, interception of communications is governed by specific legal provisions (Course 02 and Course 01 Module 7), so capture only on networks you operate, under a written policy with retention limits and access control. Seek legal advice before capturing content that could include third-party communications. Content flagged for SME review: confirm the current legal position with counsel for your jurisdiction.
