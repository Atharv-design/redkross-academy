**Playbooks are code:** keep them in version control, review changes, and test them against simulated connectors before they touch production. Test the policy logic separately from the integrations: the Tamarind engine puts every gate decision in one pure function with seven unit checks. Keep an append-only audit log of every decision (case, action, approvers, mode, result, time). Review automation metrics: time to enrich, time to contain after approval, actions blocked by gates, and false-positive containments (each one should trigger a review). Give every playbook an owner and a review date.
