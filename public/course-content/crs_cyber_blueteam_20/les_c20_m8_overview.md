## About this module

The capstone brings the course together as one engineering deliverable for the simulated enterprise Tamarind Freight. You will release a detection pack through automated gates, measure what it covers, validate it, hunt with it, and write the hunt report and an executive summary that a leadership team could act on. This module teaches the release engineering and reporting skills the capstone needs. Part C of this course contains the full brief and assessment rubric.

## Why it matters

Detection content is only valuable when it is running, trusted and understood. Releases without gates ship broken or noisy rules. Reports without evidence and denominators mislead decision-makers. And monitoring that silently stops working - as an expired certificate did at Equifax in 2017 - fails without anyone noticing.

## Where it is used

Release management of SIEM and EDR content, quarterly detection-programme reviews, hunt reports to SOC leadership, and board updates on defensive capability.

## Learning objectives

1. Structure a detection pack repository for release.
2. Define and automate release gates (tests, tags, validation, false-positive budget).
3. Manage false-positive budgets and documented exceptions.
4. Produce a manifest that makes a release verifiable.
5. Write a hunt report and an executive summary from evidence.
6. Plan operations after release: monitoring health, ownership and review.

## Before you start

**Prerequisites:** C20-M01 to M07.

**Estimated study time:** About 7 hours of lessons; the capstone itself is estimated at 16 hours (Part C).
