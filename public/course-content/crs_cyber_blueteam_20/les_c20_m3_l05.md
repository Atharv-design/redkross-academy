Suricata writes alerts and protocol records to eve.json: http, dns, tls, fileinfo, flow and more. For an investigator the protocol records are often more valuable than the alerts, because they show everything the sensor parsed, not just what a rule matched. In the lab, eve.json contained 4 alerts, 2 http records, 2 fileinfo records, 2 flow records and a stats record. The http records show the full story: a 200 for /backup/customers.csv with user agent rk-backup-agent/1.0, then a 404 for /backup/missing.zip from curl/8.23.0. Move from metadata to packets when you need content (what file was transferred), the exact sequence of a protocol exchange, or evidence for legal proceedings. Extract objects and hash them as in Course 09 Module 6.
