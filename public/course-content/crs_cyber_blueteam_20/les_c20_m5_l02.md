A good hypothesis is specific, testable and scoped. For example: "An adversary has established persistence on a Tamarind host using a scheduled task that runs a script host or a binary from a user-writable path (T1053.005), within the last 30 days." It names a behaviour, a technique, a population and a time window. Sources of hypotheses include threat intelligence (a sector advisory describing a technique), ATT&CK techniques with low coverage scores (Module 1), crown-jewel assets (who accesses the finance share?), recent incidents ("where else did the same attacker go?") and environmental changes (a new VPN, a merger). Avoid hypotheses that cannot fail ("something bad may be happening") or that the data cannot answer.
