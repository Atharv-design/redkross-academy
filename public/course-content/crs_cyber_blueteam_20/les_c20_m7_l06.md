A purple-team report for each gap gives: the case, the technique, the expected and the observed result, the root cause (rule logic, missing telemetry, parsing, sensor configuration), the fix, the owner and the retest date. Prioritise gaps on priority techniques and on any gap that blinds a whole class (missing telemetry beats a missing variant). Retest after the fix, and on a schedule, because environments drift: agents are upgraded, fields are renamed and ATT&CK is revised.
