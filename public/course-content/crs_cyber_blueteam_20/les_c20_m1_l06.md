Treat detection content like software:

- a backlog of engineering work ranked by risk (blind priority techniques first, then visible-but-undetected ones);
- version control and peer review for rules (Module 4);
- automated tests and release gates (Modules 4 and 8);
- service levels for telemetry health (for example "a host that stops sending for 30 minutes raises a ticket");
- metrics: coverage score, detection rate in purple tests, alert volume and true-positive rate per rule, mean time to detect in exercises.

Report the programme to leadership in business terms: "we can now detect credential spraying on all domain controllers" is more useful than "we added 40 rules".
