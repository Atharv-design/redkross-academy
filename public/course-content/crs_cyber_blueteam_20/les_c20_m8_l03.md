Analyst time is finite. A false-positive budget sets the maximum alert volume a rule may add (per day, or per analyst-hour) before it must be tuned, aggregated or downgraded. Tamarind's DNS rule fires 82 times on the reference day. All 82 hits belong to the real incident, but on a normal day with legitimate long TXT queries the rule could easily exceed the budget. The pack records an exception: "aggregate per client in SIEM; ticket DET-112". The SIEM then raises one alert per client per hour, and the rule's raw hits feed that aggregation. Exceptions need a reason, an owner, a ticket and an expiry, otherwise the budget is meaningless. Demonstrating the gate with the exception removed (in the lab) blocks the release.
