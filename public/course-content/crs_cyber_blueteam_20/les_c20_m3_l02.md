Useful DNS features include query volume per client; ratio of NXDOMAIN (name does not exist) responses; unusual record types (TXT, NULL) from workstations; label length and character randomness (DNS labels may be up to 63 octets); and the number of distinct names queried under one registered domain. Each feature has innocent explanations. Mistyped names cause NXDOMAIN. Security products and email authentication use long TXT labels. Content networks use many subdomains. Combinations are stronger than single features. In Tamarind's data one client (10.20.1.17, the address of WS-07) made 82 TXT queries with 28-character random first labels, all under one registered domain, cdn-sync.example. No other client made a TXT query.

DNS also gives early, cheap containment: a protective resolver can refuse to resolve a malicious domain for the whole estate at once. Use it through change control, because a wrong entry breaks business services.
