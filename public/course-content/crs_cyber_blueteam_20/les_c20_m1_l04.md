ATT&CK began as a catalogue of adversary behaviour. It now also models how to detect that behaviour. In Enterprise ATT&CK 20.1 the STIX data contains 706 detection strategies, 1,773 analytics and 106 data components [R1]. A detection strategy describes how to detect a technique. An analytic is a platform-specific description of the logic. A data component names the observable it needs (for example process creation, or a command's execution). The older "data source" objects are no longer present as live objects. Use these objects as starting points, not as finished rules. They tell you what to collect and what to look for, and you still have to write, test and tune the logic for your own environment.

ATT&CK is also a shared vocabulary. Tagging every rule with technique IDs lets you compute coverage, compare with threat reports and talk to other teams. The vocabulary changes: in 2026 (version 19, according to secondary reporting) the old Defense Evasion tactic was split into Stealth (TA0005) and Defense Impairment (TA0112), and some techniques were revoked and re-issued with new IDs. For example, Clear Windows Event Logs moved from T1070.001 to T1685.005. Mapping must be versioned and maintained (Module 7).
