​### Objectives

Demonstrate LO1-LO8 by engineering, validating and releasing a detection pack for the simulated enterprise Tamarind Freight, and by reporting a hunt and the incident to technical and executive audiences.

​### Scenario

You join Tamarind Freight (fictional) as its first detection engineer. The day after the 2026-11-03 incident on WS-07, the CISO asks for three things: a trustworthy detection pack that would catch the same activity and its likely variants; evidence of what the SOC can and cannot see; and a hunt for anything similar elsewhere. Your instructor provides either the standard dataset (make_telemetry.py) or a variant generated by changing the storyline in a copy of the generator.

​### Requirements

```text
R1 Coverage: run coverage.py before and after your work; add a
   fleet-weighted score (CT2); explain every change.
R2 Endpoint and network analysis: produce the WS-07 timeline (process,
   identity, DNS, flow) with sources and inferences marked.
R3 Detections: add at least two new Sigma rules to the pack (one must
   close a "visible but no rule" gap), each with positive, negative and
   edge tests; sigma check must report no issues; convert at least one
   rule to a backend and comment on the output.
R4 IDS: add one tested Suricata rule for the lab capture (flowbits or a
   transactional rule).
R5 Hunts: run at least two hypothesis-driven hunts with data requirements,
   coverage statements and seeded validation; grade all findings.
R6 Automation: design one playbook with an automation ladder, approval
   gates (two approvers for tier 0), rate limit, input validation and
   rollback; demonstrate the gate logic with soar.py or your own tests.
R7 Validation: extend purple.py with at least three new variant cases
   derived from vendor documentation; tune and prove no regression.
R8 Release and report: release with build_pack.py (all gates pass, any
   exception documented); deliver the manifest, a hunt report and a
   one-page executive summary.
```

​### Deliverables

Detection repository (rules, tests, tuned rules, Suricata rule), coverage outputs, timeline, hunt report, playbook design, purple-team results, pack/manifest.txt and release_report.txt, an executive summary, and a one-page reflection on the biggest remaining gap and how you would close it.
