A hunt report (technical audience):

- hypotheses (each in testable form) and the trigger for the hunt;
- scope, time window and data sources, with coverage (n/N hosts);
- method (queries or scripts, versioned) and validation performed;
- findings, each graded with evidence and confidence;
- gaps (what could not be seen) and their impact on conclusions;
- recommendations: detections, telemetry fixes, baselines and incidents.

An executive summary (leadership audience), on one page:

- what happened or what was tested, in business terms;
- impact and confidence;
- what has been done;
- decisions or resources needed, with options and costs;
- how progress will be measured (for example, the coverage score with denominators, and the purple-team detection rate).

Avoid jargon such as technique IDs on the executive page; keep them in the technical report. Never overstate certainty: "no evidence found on 4 of 16 hosts" is not "no compromise".
