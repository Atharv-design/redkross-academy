Providers offer managed detection that analyses their own telemetry - for example Amazon GuardDuty and Security Hub in AWS, and Microsoft Defender for Cloud, Defender XDR and Microsoft Sentinel in Azure. Use them because they see provider-side signals and maintain threat intelligence; complement them with custom rules for your organisation's context (admin roles, approved regions, break-glass accounts) and route all findings to one triage queue with severity mapping. Enable them organisation-wide with a delegated administrator account, and test that findings reach the SOC.
