## About this module

Detection engineering is the discipline of designing, building, testing, deploying and maintaining the logic that turns telemetry into alerts. It treats detections like software: written from requirements, version controlled, peer reviewed, tested against known-good and known-bad data, and measured in production. This module teaches how to reason about detection logic, how to write portable rules in the Sigma format, and how to map detections and gaps to MITRE ATT&CK.

## Why it matters

Every alert an analyst triages (Module 4) was created by someone's detection logic. Weak logic produces noise or blind spots; good logic gives analysts alerts that are explainable, high-fidelity and resilient to small changes by the attacker.

## Where it is used

SOC content teams, MDR providers, security product vendors, purple teams (Course 19 discusses collaboration with offensive teams).

## Learning objectives

1. Describe the detection engineering lifecycle.
2. Choose between signature, behavioural, threshold, anomaly and correlation logic.
3. Write, validate and convert Sigma rules.
4. Test detections with positive and negative cases.
5. Map detections and data sources to ATT&CK and identify gaps.
6. Document and maintain detections.
