- Indicators are perishable; attackers rotate infrastructure, sometimes per victim.
- Shared infrastructure (cloud, CDN, VPN providers, Tor exits) causes collateral matches.
- Feeds overlap and repeat each other, making one report look like many confirmations ("circular reporting").
- Attribution is hard and often unnecessary for defence; act on behaviour.
- Absence of matches proves nothing if the adversary is new to you or your telemetry cannot see the indicator type.
- Vendor naming differs: one actor can have many names across vendors; use crosswalks (MITRE ATT&CK group pages list associated names).
- Bias: analysts may over-weight dramatic reports or vendor marketing.

State confidence in your own conclusions explicitly (for example "moderate confidence the spray and phishing are the same actor - shared timing, no shared infrastructure").
