**Levels:**

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Strategic | trends and threats for leaders (which actors target our sector, why). |
| Operational | campaigns and their timing (a phishing wave against Indian microfinance firms this month). |
| Tactical | TTPs and indicators analysts and tools can act on. |

**The intelligence cycle:** direction (what questions do we need answered - priority intelligence requirements), collection, processing, analysis, dissemination, feedback. A SOC that consumes feeds without stating its requirements collects noise.
