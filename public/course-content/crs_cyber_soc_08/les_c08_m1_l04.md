**Start of shift:** read the handover; check open cases, SIEM health (are all log sources reporting?), threat intelligence updates and change calendars.

**During:** work the queue by priority; document as you go; escalate early when impact may be high; follow playbooks but think.

**End of shift:** write a handover - open cases, decisions pending, ongoing incidents, platform issues, notable intelligence (Module 8 shift report).

**Documentation habits:** UTC timestamps, exact queries used, evidence references, facts separated from hypotheses.
