NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3, published in April 2025, is titled "Incident Response Recommendations and Considerations for Cybersecurity Risk Management: A CSF 2.0 Community Profile". It supersedes Rev. 2 of August 2012 [R1]. Rev. 2 described a lifecycle of preparation; detection and analysis; containment, eradication and recovery; and post-incident activity. Rev. 3 instead integrates incident response into organisation-wide cybersecurity risk management as described by CSF 2.0, whose functions are Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond and Recover (Course 01 Module 4). The older lifecycle terms remain common in playbooks and are used in this module as plain vocabulary.

**Roles you will meet:**

- incident commander or lead (decisions and coordination)
- SOC analysts (detection, scoping, evidence)
- IT operations (executing containment)
- forensics (Course 09)
- legal and compliance (obligations, privilege)
- communications/PR, executives
- external parties: CERT-In, law enforcement, insurers, IR retainers
