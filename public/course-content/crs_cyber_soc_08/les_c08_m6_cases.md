## ATT&CK's 2026 restructuring - mappings age ------------------------------------------------------------------ Context: MITRE's 2026 ATT&CK releases split the long-standing Defense Evasion tactic into Stealth (TA0005) and Defense Impairment (TA0112), and moved several techniques; for example "Clear Windows Event Logs" now appears as T1685.005 (created 14 April 2026) under Defense Impairment, and the T1070 Indicator Removal sub-technique list on the current site begins at T1070.003 [C08-M06-R2] [C08-M06-R3]. Lessons: detection tags, coverage heat maps and reports that cite technique IDs must be versioned and periodically re-mapped; automated tooling that validates tags against an older ATT&CK dataset may accept or reject IDs inconsistently; training content (including this course) states the version used. (Details of every change are in MITRE's release notes; consult them rather than relying on this summary.)
