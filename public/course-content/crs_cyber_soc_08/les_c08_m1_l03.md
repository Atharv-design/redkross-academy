```text
1. Generation      a rule, model or analyst creates an alert.
2. Queueing        prioritised by severity and asset criticality.
3. Triage          is it real? what is the scope? (Module 4)
4. Investigation   timeline, related events, affected entities.
5. Disposition     true positive (malicious), benign true positive
                   (correct detection of authorised activity), false
                   positive (rule fired incorrectly), or inconclusive.
6. Escalation /    incident declared and handed to response (Module 7).
   response
7. Closure         documented with evidence and reasoning.
8. Feedback        tuning rules, updating playbooks, improving telemetry.
```

Every closed alert should answer: what happened, what evidence supports the conclusion, what was done, and what should change.
