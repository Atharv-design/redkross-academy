**Communication principles:**

- Use out-of-band channels if email or chat may be compromised.
- Share on a need-to-know basis, and keep a single incident log of decisions and times.
- Separate facts from hypotheses.
- Coordinate external statements through legal and communications.
- Do not alert the attacker. For example, do not email a compromised mailbox about the incident.

India-specific obligations (summary only; Course 01 Module 7 gives the detail and SME-review notes):

- CERT-In Directions of 28 April 2022 [R2]: report specified cyber incidents within 6 hours of noticing them or being notified of them; keep ICT logs for 180 days within India; synchronise clocks to NTP sources specified by CERT-In (NIC or NPL), or to traceable sources.
- DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025: Rule 7 breach intimation requires notifying affected Data Principals and the Board without delay, plus a detailed report to the Board within 72 hours. It falls in the tranche expected to take effect 18 months after the Rules were notified (13 November 2025), so check commencement at the time of the incident.
- Sector regulators (for example RBI for regulated entities), contractual notices and insurer notification may also apply.

The SOC's role is to give legal and the incident lead accurate facts and times quickly, including when the incident was "noticed". The SOC does not make the legal decision itself.
