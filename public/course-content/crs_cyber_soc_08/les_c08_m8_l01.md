Threat hunting is proactive, human-led searching for threats that evaded existing detections. It is not: running the same alert queries again; browsing logs without a question; or penetration testing (hunters look at defensive data, they do not attack systems). Prerequisites: good telemetry (Module 2), a searchable platform (Module 3), knowledge of normal (baselines), and time protected from the alert queue. Many teams describe hunt maturity from "relies on automated alerts only" to "routinely creates and automates new analytics from hunts".
