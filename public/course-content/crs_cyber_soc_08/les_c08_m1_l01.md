MITRE's guide "11 Strategies of a World-Class Cybersecurity Operations Center" describes a SOC as a team primarily composed of security analysts organised to detect, analyse, respond to, report on and prevent cybersecurity incidents [R1]. Core functions:

- Monitoring and triage of alerts.
- Incident analysis and coordination of response.
- Threat intelligence use (Module 5).
- Detection engineering and tuning (Module 6).
- Threat hunting (Module 8).
- Vulnerability and exposure awareness feeding risk decisions.
- Reporting to leadership and regulators.

A SOC sits within the NIST CSF 2.0 Detect and Respond Functions, but depends on Identify (asset inventory, Course 01 Module 1) and Protect (controls, Course 01 Module 6) to know what matters.
