The executive report is for decision-makers. It is short, clear and honest:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| scope/authorisation | what was tested, under whose authorisation, in what isolated scope. |
| outcome | how many techniques were emulated and the DETECTION COVERAGE (e.g. 4/7), in plain terms. |
| risk | what the coverage means for the organisation's ability to detect a real adversary. |
| top recommendations | the few highest-value actions (usually the top detection gaps to close), prioritised. |

It avoids jargon and never overstates: coverage is reported as measured, with the gaps named. Executives make resourcing and risk decisions from this, so clarity and honesty matter more than technical depth. The lab's report leads with exactly this summary.
