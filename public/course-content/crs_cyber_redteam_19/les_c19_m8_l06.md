The capstone deliverable is a complete, isolated engagement with both reports:

1. scope and authorisation (RoE gate passed; isolated; simulation-only);
2. the validated, in-scope, threat-informed emulation plan;
3. the emulation run with evidence (chain of custody);
4. the technique-to-telemetry/containment mapping;
5. the purple-team gap analysis (coverage, gaps, retest list);
6. an EXECUTIVE report (risk + coverage + top recommendations) and a TECHNICAL report (per-technique detail + retest list), every statement evidence-traced.

Producing this demonstrates the entire course: you can engineer, plan, run (safely and in isolation), measure and report a red team engagement that leaves the organisation measurably better at detecting real adversaries. The lab's report.py assembles the two reports from the artifacts.
