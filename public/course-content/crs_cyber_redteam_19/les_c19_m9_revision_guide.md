This course teaches red team operations as a GOVERNANCE, ADVERSARY-EMULATION- PLANNING and DETECTION discipline, from the defender's seat. It contains no exploit code, no payloads and no attack commands. Every lab tool is OFFLINE and reads a file you own; adversary emulation is bounded to an ISOLATED, OWNED range using lab accounts and synthetic data. The unifying principles: AUTHORISE and SCOPE everything; EMULATE the relevant adversary within SIMULATION BOUNDARIES; map every technique to a DETECTION; MEASURE coverage and close gaps with the blue team; and REPORT honestly.

**Module 1 (Engagement engineering):** authorisation (written permission from the right party), objectives, the rules of engagement (scope, window, limits, deconfliction, stop conditions, data handling, reporting) and safety controls (isolation, no destruction, no real data, simulation-only). The engagement is GATED on a complete RoE. roe.py: incomplete RoE 10 findings/6 HIGH FAIL -> complete RoE PASS.

**Module 2 (Adversary emulation):** threat-informed planning chooses WHAT to emulate from the organisation's threats; MITRE ATT&CK (Enterprise v20.1) gives the shared language of tactics and techniques; technique selection spans the tactics and targets detection gaps; simulation boundaries limit HOW. emulation_plan.py validates the 7-step plan (in scope, safety-bounded, detection-mapped) -> PASS.

**Module 3 (Infrastructure assessment):** map assets and identities in scope; enforce scope FAIL-SAFE (anything not provably in scope is refused); validate findings minimally and non-destructively; manage evidence with a tamper-evident CHAIN OF CUSTODY. scopecheck.py refuses out-of-scope (8.8.8.8); evidence.py chains entries by hash (intact -> OK; one-char edit -> BROKEN).

Module 4 (Endpoint and identity testing): assess privilege boundaries and credential exposure; the identity ATT&CK techniques (T1078 Valid Accounts, T1087 Account Discovery, T1550 Use Alternate Authentication Material) are "living off the land", so detection is BEHAVIOURAL over authentication/directory telemetry, paired with containment (MFA, tiering, least privilege). attackmap.py maps each to telemetry, detection and containment.

Module 5 (Lateral movement concepts): lateral movement (TA0008) abuses TRUST relationships (reused local admin, over-privileged service accounts, flat networks); SEGMENTATION VALIDATION confirms paths that should be blocked are; each hop is a DETECTION OPPORTUNITY. attackmap.py (Lateral Movement) maps T1021 and T1550 to behavioural detections and segmentation/credential containment. Treated conceptually - no intrusion how-to.

Module 6 (Command and control concepts): C2 (TA0011) is the adversary's lifeline that must cross the NETWORK BOUNDARY; its INDICATORS (beaconing periodicity, destination, protocol anomaly) are detected behaviourally and CONTAINED by egress filtering and proxy inspection. attackmap.py (C2) maps T1071. The lab emulates only the INDICATOR to a LAB SINK (no real egress); there is no C2 tooling.

Module 7 (Purple team operations): red and blue COLLABORATE - emulate, measure, fix, re-emulate. Detection coverage = detected/emulated; a GAP is a technique with no/inadequate detection; retesting closes it; continuous validation catches drift. purple.py: 4/7 coverage; gaps T1087, T1550, T1005; retest list.

**Module 8 (Capstone):** run the whole engagement in a fully isolated range and deliver an EXECUTIVE report (scope, coverage 4/7=57%, risk, top recommendations) and a TECHNICAL report (per-technique DETECTED/GAP, containment, retest list), every statement traced to evidence. report.py assembles both.

**Tool-to-module map (lab):** roe.py (M01); emulation_plan.py (M02); scopecheck.py + evidence.py (M03); attackmap.py (M04-M06, with --tactic filters); purple.py (M07); report.py (M08).
