Endpoint and identity testing must be authorised and safe:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| authorised/in scope | only in-scope hosts and lab accounts; never harvest real users' credentials. |
| non-destructive | read privilege and exposure state; do not lock out accounts or disrupt authentication. |
| isolated/simulation | emulate identity techniques in the isolated range with lab credentials and synthetic data (this course's lab is offline and simulation-only). |
| data care | credentials and identity data are sensitive personal data; handle per the RoE and the DPDP Act, 2023. |

The goal is to assess and improve identity defence, not to amass credentials. Mishandled, credential testing can cause lockouts or expose real secrets, so the boundaries matter. This is not legal advice.
