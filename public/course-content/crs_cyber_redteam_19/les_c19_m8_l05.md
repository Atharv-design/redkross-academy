Even in delivery, the course's rules hold:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| isolation | the exercise ran in a fully isolated range, on lab accounts and synthetic data; the report says so. |
| scope | only authorised, in-scope systems were involved; the report records the scope and that nothing out of scope was touched. |
| data care | any sensitive data is handled per the RoE and the DPDP Act, 2023; the report does not leak secrets. |
| honesty | the report is truthful about what was and was not achieved and about coverage. |

Delivery is where the engagement's professionalism is judged. A safe, scoped, honest exercise produces a report the organisation can trust and act on. In India, the whole engagement's lawfulness rests on the authorisation and scope the report documents (IT Act, 2000). This is not legal advice.
