## About this module

The capstone runs the whole engagement end to end, in a fully isolated range, and delivers the reports. It integrates everything: the RoE gate, the threat-informed emulation plan, scope enforcement and evidence, technique-to-detection mapping, purple-team gap analysis, and - finally - an EXECUTIVE and a TECHNICAL report. You will run the lab's report assembler, which turns the engagement artifacts into both reports, and you will produce the capstone deliverable.

## Why it matters

An engagement delivers value only through its reporting: decision-makers need an honest executive summary of risk and detection coverage, and engineers need the technical detail and the retest list. Running a fully isolated exercise and delivering clear, evidence-backed executive and technical reports is the culmination of the discipline - and the proof that you can conduct red team operations safely, lawfully and usefully.

## Where it is used

Engagement delivery and reporting; executive briefings and technical debriefs; the hand-off from red/purple teaming to detection engineering and risk management; and the record that supports audit and improvement.

## Learning objectives

1. Integrate the whole engagement into one isolated exercise.
2. Assemble an executive report (risk, coverage, recommendations).
3. Assemble a technical report (per-technique detail, retest list).
4. Trace every statement to engagement evidence.
5. Deliver the capstone within authorisation and isolation.
6. Produce a complete engagement deliverable.

## Before you start

**Prerequisites:** Modules 1-7.

**Estimated study time:** About 11 hours.
