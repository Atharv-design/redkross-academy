## About this module

Endpoints and identities are where most intrusions turn an initial foothold into real access. This module assesses privilege boundaries and credential exposure, and - centrally - relates each tested behaviour to the DEFENSIVE TELEMETRY that should detect it. You will use the lab's ATT&CK map to turn the endpoint/identity steps of the emulation plan into a table of data sources, detections and containment.

## Why it matters

Attackers abuse valid accounts and weak privilege boundaries because these are quiet - they look like legitimate activity. The defender's job is to make that abuse

**VISIBLE:** collect the right endpoint and identity telemetry, and build detections for anomalous logons, privilege misuse and credential exposure. Assessing privilege boundaries and credential exposure, and mapping each to telemetry, is how an engagement improves endpoint and identity defence rather than merely finding a way through.

## Where it is used

Identity and endpoint security assessment; privilege-tiering reviews; credential-hygiene programmes; and EDR/SIEM detection engineering for identity threats (ties to Course 13 Active Directory and Course 20 Blue Team).

## Learning objectives

1. Explain privilege boundaries and how they are assessed.
2. Explain credential exposure and its review.
3. Explain the identity-relevant ATT&CK techniques (e.g. T1078).
4. Map endpoint/identity techniques to defensive telemetry and detection.
5. Explain containment for identity threats.
6. Keep endpoint/identity testing authorised and safe.

## Before you start
