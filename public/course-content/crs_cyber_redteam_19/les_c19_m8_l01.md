The capstone integrates every stage into one isolated exercise:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| gate | the RoE gate passes (Module 1) - authorised, scoped, safety-bounded. |
| plan | a threat-informed, ATT&CK-mapped emulation plan is validated (Module 2), in scope (Module 3). |
| run | techniques are emulated in the isolated range under simulation boundaries; evidence is recorded with chain of custody (Module 3). |
| map | each technique is mapped to telemetry and containment (Modules 4-6). |
| measure | purple-team gap analysis produces coverage, gaps and a retest list (Module 7). |
| report | the executive and technical reports are assembled (this module). |

The integration is the point: the engagement is a disciplined pipeline from authorisation to measured improvement, every step bounded and evidenced. The lab's report.py consumes the engagement artifacts (RoE, plan, telemetry) and produces both reports.
