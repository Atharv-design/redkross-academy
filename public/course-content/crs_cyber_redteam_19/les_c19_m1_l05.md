Engagement engineering ends with a GATE: the engagement may not begin until the RoE is complete. The lab's roe.py implements this gate - it reviews an RoE file against the required controls (authorisation, scope, window, contacts, deconfliction, stop conditions, out-of-scope, data handling, isolation, no-destruction, no-real-data, simulation-only, reporting) and returns FAIL (with findings) or PASS. An incomplete RoE fails on multiple HIGH findings and the engagement does not start; a complete RoE passes. This makes governance a checkable precondition, not a hopeful intention - the same enforcing discipline used throughout the library's labs, applied to the start of an engagement. The deep dive runs both an incomplete and a complete RoE.
