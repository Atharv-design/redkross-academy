The disciplines combine to keep the assessment credible and safe:

```text
map only in scope; enforce scope before every action (fail-safe);
validate findings with minimal, non-destructive, reversible proof;
record every action with attribution, time and integrity;
store evidence per the RoE and keep the chain of custody intact.
```

Together these ensure the engagement proves real risk without causing harm or straying out of bounds, and produces evidence the organisation can trust. They are the backbone of the later technique-focused modules, which always operate inside this frame. In India, staying in authorised scope is also what keeps the work lawful (IT Act, 2000); evidence integrity supports any later legal use. This is not legal advice.
