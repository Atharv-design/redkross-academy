Chain of custody is the unbroken, documented history of evidence - who recorded what, when, and that it has not changed. Integrity is enforced cryptographically: in the lab's evidence log, each entry includes the hash of the previous entry and a hash of its own content, forming a chain. 'verify' recomputes the chain; if any entry was altered or removed, the recomputed hash will not match and the chain reports BROKEN. This is the same principle as a hash chain or a forensic hash (Course 09): you cannot change history without detection. Chain of custody turns a pile of notes into evidence that stands up to scrutiny. The deep dive shows the chain intact and then detects a tamper.
