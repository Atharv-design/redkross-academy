Controlled validation confirms a finding with the MINIMUM necessary action and no collateral damage:

```text
minimal proof   demonstrate a weakness exists with the least intrusive evidence
                (e.g. show that access is possible, not ransack the system).
non-destructive never delete, corrupt or disrupt to prove a point.
reversible      any change is tracked and undone; state is restored.
in scope        validation happens only against in-scope assets.
```

Controlled validation is the professional alternative to "popping the box" - it proves the risk without becoming the incident. It mirrors the retest discipline from earlier courses (prove the finding, then prove the fix). In this course's lab, validation is modelled through the review tools and the evidence log; no real system is exploited.
