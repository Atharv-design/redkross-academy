This module is deliberately CONCEPTUAL and defensive:

```text
no how-to     it does not provide procedures or tooling for moving through a
              network; it explains the trust abused and the detection/containment.
isolated      any emulation of these techniques happens only in the isolated
              range, on lab hosts and accounts, under the simulation boundaries of
              Module 2 - this course's lab is offline and simulation-only.
defensive aim the purpose is to validate segmentation and build detections, not to
              practise intrusion.
```

Treating lateral movement this way keeps the material safe and aligned with the course's defender-first stance, while still teaching what matters: where trust is too broad and how each hop can be detected and contained. Unauthorised movement through systems you do not own is unlawful (IT Act, 2000); the lab stays in the owned range. This is not legal advice.
