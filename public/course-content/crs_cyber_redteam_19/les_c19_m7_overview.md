## About this module

Purple teaming is where red and blue work TOGETHER: the red team emulates techniques, the blue team measures what they detect, and together they close the gaps. This module covers red/blue collaboration, detection-gap analysis, and retesting. You will run the lab's purple-team analysis, which compares the emulated techniques with the detections achieved and produces the gaps and the retest list - the deliverable that turns emulation into better detection.

## Why it matters

The point of adversary emulation is not to "win" against the blue team but to IMPROVE detection. Purple teaming makes that explicit: every emulated technique is checked against what the blue team saw, each miss becomes a detection to engineer, and the fix is confirmed by re-emulating. This collaborative, measured cycle is the most valuable output of the whole course - it is how an engagement leaves an organisation measurably better at detecting real adversaries.

## Where it is used

Purple-team exercises and continuous validation programmes; detection engineering and coverage measurement (ATT&CK coverage); and the feedback loop between red teams/emulation and the SOC/detection engineering (Courses 12 and 20).

## Learning objectives

1. Explain purple teaming and red/blue collaboration.
2. Explain detection coverage and gap analysis.
3. Analyse emulation vs detection to find gaps.
4. Build a retest list and the retest cycle.
5. Explain how gaps become engineered detections.
6. Explain continuous validation.
