Purple teaming depends on red and blue collaborating openly:

```text
shared plan      both sides know the ATT&CK techniques being emulated (from Module
                 2) and the detections each should trigger.
shared telemetry the blue team reports what they saw for each technique; the red
                 team reports what they did.
shared language  ATT&CK gives both sides the same vocabulary, so a "gap" is a named
                 technique with no detection, not a vague feeling.
no blame         the goal is improvement; a gap is a finding to fix, not a failure
                 to hide.
```

Collaboration is what makes the exercise efficient and honest: instead of the red team hiding and the blue team guessing, both work the same list. The engagement's deconfliction and RoE (Module 1) support this. The lab models the shared artifacts: one emulation plan, one telemetry record, one gap analysis.
