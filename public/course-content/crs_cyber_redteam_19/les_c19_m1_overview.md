## About this module

Before a single technique is emulated, a red team engagement must be ENGINEERED: authorised, scoped, bounded by rules of engagement, and protected by safety controls. This module covers authorisation and objectives, the rules of engagement (RoE), and the safety controls that keep the work legitimate and non-destructive. You will run the lab's RoE validator, which gates an engagement - failing an incomplete RoE and passing a complete one - so that nothing begins until the governance is in place.

## Why it matters

A test that is not properly authorised, scoped and contained is not a security exercise - it is a liability and, without authorisation, a crime. The difference between professional red teaming and illegal hacking is entirely governance: written authorisation, a clear scope, agreed rules, stop conditions, deconfliction and safety controls. Engineering the engagement is therefore the single most important skill in this course; everything else is done inside its boundaries.

## Where it is used

Scoping and contracting of penetration tests and red team engagements; rules of engagement and authorisation letters; safety and deconfliction planning; and the governance that internal red teams and external firms operate under (aligned to NIST SP 800-115 planning).

## Learning objectives

1. Explain authorisation and engagement objectives.
2. Explain the rules of engagement and their required contents.
3. Explain safety controls (isolation, no-destruction, no-real-data).
4. Explain stop conditions, deconfliction and emergency contacts.
5. Gate an engagement on a complete RoE.
6. Explain the legal and ethical basis of authorised testing.
