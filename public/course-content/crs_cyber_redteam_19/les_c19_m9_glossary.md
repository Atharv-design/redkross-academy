authorisation  written permission to test defined systems in a defined window. rules of engagement (RoE)  the agreed contract for how the engagement runs. safety control  an engineering measure that prevents harm (isolation, etc.). deconfliction  distinguishing the test from a real attack (operator tags). stop condition  a predefined trigger to halt immediately. adversary emulation  reproducing a relevant threat's behaviour to test defences. ATT&CK tactic/technique  an adversary goal / a specific behaviour to achieve it. simulation boundary  a safety limit on how a technique is emulated. scope enforcement  acting only on provably in-scope targets (fail-safe). controlled validation  minimal, non-destructive, reversible proof of a finding. chain of custody  the unbroken, integrity-protected history of evidence. privilege boundary  a separation between access levels (user/admin, tiers). credential exposure  any way a secret becomes available to an attacker. lateral movement  moving from a foothold to other hosts/accounts toward a goal. segmentation validation  confirming paths that should be blocked are blocked. command and control (C2)  the adversary's channel to direct compromised systems. beacon  a regular, machine-like check-in from an implant to a C2 server. purple teaming  red and blue collaborating to improve detection. detection coverage  the fraction of relevant techniques detectable. detection gap  a technique with no or inadequate detection. retesting  re-emulating a technique to confirm a new detection fires.
