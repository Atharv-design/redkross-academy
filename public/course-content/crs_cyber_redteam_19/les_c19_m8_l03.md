The technical report is for engineers and the blue team. It is detailed and actionable:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| per technique | each emulated technique, its ATT&CK ID and tactic, the safety boundary used, and whether it was DETECTED (with the alert) or a GAP (with the detection needed). |
| containment | the containment for each technique. |
| retest list | the prioritised gaps to engineer and re-emulate (Module 7). |
| evidence | references to the evidence (chain of custody) backing each finding. |

This is the document the blue team works from to close gaps and the record for retesting. It is precise, technique-by-technique, and tied to the ATT&CK mapping. The lab's report's technical section provides this per-step detail and the retest list.
