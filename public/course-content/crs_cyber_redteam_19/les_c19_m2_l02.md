Threat-informed planning chooses WHAT to emulate from an understanding of the threats the organisation faces:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| threat profile | which adversaries or threat types are relevant (by sector, geography, technology, and threat intelligence). |
| behaviours | the techniques those adversaries are known to use (from ATT&CK and threat reporting). |
| priorities | emulate the behaviours that matter most for the organisation's risk and detection gaps. |

This makes the engagement relevant and efficient, and it aligns red and blue around the same threat model (threat-informed defence). The lab uses a generic teaching profile; a real plan would be built from the organisation's actual threat intelligence. Planning also sets objectives (Module 1) and the detections to test.
