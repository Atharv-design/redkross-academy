## About this module

The capstone draws the whole course into one piece of work: build a contained Active Directory lab, harden its identity controls against everything you have learned, and produce an audit report with evidence of before-and-after state. You have already built the pieces across Modules 1-7 - a domain controller, a tiered organisation, the attack-path and monitoring tools, and an assessment method. Here you assemble them into a deliverable you could put in front of an auditor or a CISO: a hardened domain and a report that proves it.

## Why it matters

Employers and auditors do not want to hear that you understand Active Directory security; they want to see a hardened directory and evidence. The capstone is the rehearsal: a scoped build, a documented set of hardening changes each tied to a control and a risk, and a report whose findings are closed and re-tested. It is the same artefact an identity-security engineer produces in the first ninety days of a real role.

## Where it is used

Identity security hardening projects, audit preparation and response, onboarding into an identity or security engineering role, and security-programme reporting to leadership.

## Learning objectives

1. Build and document a contained AD lab with an authorised scope.
2. Harden identity controls across authentication, administration and monitoring.
3. Evidence each hardening change against a control and a risk.
4. Run a before-and-after assessment and prove closure.
5. Write an audit report with findings, actions and verification.
6. Present residual risk and a forward plan honestly.

## Before you start

**Prerequisites:** Modules 1-7.

**Estimated study time:** About 16 hours.
