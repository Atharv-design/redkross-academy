Promoting a server to a DC ("provisioning" in Samba, "dcpromo" historically on Windows) creates the directory database, the SYSVOL share (which holds Group Policy and logon scripts), the DNS zones and the Kerberos realm. In the lab, dc/provision.sh provisions the realm AD.SAHYADRI.EXAMPLE with the Samba internal DNS back end and then runs the directory service in the foreground. Once a DC is running, it can describe itself: samba-tool domain info (or, on Windows, Get-ADDomain and Get-ADForest) returns the domain, forest, NetBIOS name, DC host name and version. Verifying a new DC this way - and checking that DNS SRV records resolve - is the first thing to do before building anything on top of it.
