## About this module

This module is about running the directory: creating and organising users, groups and organisational units; applying settings at scale with Group Policy; setting password and lockout policy, including fine-grained policies for sensitive accounts; and governing privileged accounts so that power is granted deliberately and reviewed. You will build the Sahyadri Logistics organisation in the lab - a tiered set of organisational units, users, groups, a service account with a Service Principal Name, a fine-grained password policy and a Group Policy object - and read the resulting policy back from the directory.

## Why it matters

Most real-world identity weaknesses are administrative, not exploitative: a group whose membership nobody reviews, a password policy that is too weak or applied too broadly, a service account treated like furniture. Good administration is a security control. The structures you build here - organisational units, groups, policies - are the surfaces on which the tiered model (Module 4) and the attack-path defences (Module 5) are applied.

## Where it is used

Daily operations in every AD environment: joiner/mover/leaver processes, help-desk account management, Group Policy baselines for workstations and servers, password and lockout standards, and the governance of administrative groups.

## Learning objectives

1. Create and use organisational units to structure administration and policy.
2. Distinguish user, group and computer objects and the group scopes and types.
3. Explain Group Policy: GPOs, links, inheritance and processing order.
4. Configure domain and fine-grained password and lockout policies.
5. Govern privileged accounts: least privilege, separate admin accounts, and review.
6. Read applied policy and group membership back from the directory.
