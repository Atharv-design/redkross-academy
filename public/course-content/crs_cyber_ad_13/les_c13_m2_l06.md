**Putting it together:** a user logs on, the AS issues a TGT (AES), the client later presents the TGT to the TGS to get a service ticket for a named SPN, and the service validates it. A well-instrumented directory records each step as an audit event with the account, the source address, the result and the encryption type. In the lab you will obtain a TGT with kinit, use it to bind to LDAP over GSSAPI (proving the ticket works without a password), and read the matching JSON audit event the DC emitted - including the eventId, the logon type, the status and the encryption type. That record is the raw material of Module 6's monitoring.
