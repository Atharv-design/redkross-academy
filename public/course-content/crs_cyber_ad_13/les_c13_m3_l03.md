Group Policy applies settings to users and computers at scale. A Group Policy Object (GPO) is a named collection of settings stored partly in the directory and partly in SYSVOL (the share created when the domain was provisioned, Module 1). A GPO has no effect until it is LINKED to a site, domain or OU. Processing order is "LSDOU": Local policy, then Site, then Domain, then OU (and nested OUs in order), with later winning on conflict - subject to "Enforced" links and "Block Inheritance" on OUs. Computer settings apply at start-up and refresh periodically; user settings apply at logon. Security-relevant baselines (password rules at the domain, audit settings, service and firewall configuration) are delivered this way. Microsoft publishes security baselines as GPO templates in the Security Compliance Toolkit (Module 4) [R3]. In the lab you will create a GPO ("RK Workstation Baseline") and list it from the directory; note that Group Policy tooling needs the DC's DNS to resolve (Module 1).
