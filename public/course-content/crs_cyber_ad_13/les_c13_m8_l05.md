The report is written for two readers: a technical reviewer who will check your evidence, and leadership who will fund the forward plan. Structure:

1. Scope and authorisation (your contained lab).
2. Method (the tools and the assessment loop, Module 7).
3. Findings before hardening (register, scored, with evidence).
4. Actions taken (each change, control reference, before/after evidence).
5. Findings after hardening (re-test, closure proven).
6. Residual risk and forward plan (what a single container cannot do - physical PAW separation, a second DC, LAPS on real hosts).

Write plainly; put evidence in an appendix; lead with the risk picture, not the tool names.
