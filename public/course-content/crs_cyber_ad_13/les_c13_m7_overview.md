## About this module

This module brings the course together into a repeatable assessment: a structured review of a directory's configuration and access, producing a prioritised, evidence-backed remediation plan. You will combine the two lab tools - dir_review.py for configuration and hygiene, auth_monitor.py for activity - with a review method and a scoring approach, and produce the kind of findings register and remediation plan that an audit (Module 8's capstone) delivers. The emphasis is on method and evidence: a finding without evidence and a fix without a priority are both useless.

## Why it matters

Organisations are assessed - by internal audit, by external auditors, by regulators, after incidents - and defenders are expected to assess themselves continuously. A good assessment finds the paths of Module 5 before an attacker does, measures against the baselines of Module 4, and produces a plan leadership can fund and track. A bad assessment is a pile of tool output no one can act on. This module teaches the difference.

## Where it is used

Internal security reviews and self-assessments, external audits and penetration-test de-briefs, identity security posture management programmes, regulatory examinations (for example in regulated finance and insurance), and post-incident reviews.

## Learning objectives

1. Plan an Active Directory assessment with an agreed, authorised scope.
2. Run a configuration and hygiene review and interpret the findings.
3. Run an access review of privileged and sensitive groups.
4. Score and prioritise findings by risk and effort.
5. Write a remediation plan with owners, evidence and verification.
6. Re-test to confirm closure and measure improvement over time.

## Before you start

**Prerequisites:** Modules 1-6; Course 17 (GRC concepts, optional but helpful).

**Estimated study time:** About 8 hours.
