A remediation plan turns findings into tracked work. Each item needs:

- the finding and its evidence (the tool output, the account, the date);
- the risk, in business terms;
- the fix (specific: "move legacy-svc to a gMSA; remove the HTTP SPN");
- an owner and a due date proportionate to the severity;
- a verification step (how closure will be proven - usually a re-run of the tool showing the finding gone).

Group quick wins (disable dormant accounts) separately from projects (deploy LAPS; roll out PAWs). The plan is the deliverable leadership funds and tracks; the capstone (Module 8) produces one.
