The capstone deliverable is a complete wireless-and-mobile assessment:

1. scope and authorisation (authorised, offline, do-no-harm);
2. method and tools (the lab reviewers);
3. findings by area - Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile app, mobile API, rogue APs - with severities;
4. enforcing evidence - the FAIL-to-PASS review outputs for the three control domains;
5. remediation mapped to the secure baseline, and verification (re-run to PASS);
6. a clear summary and the detailed evidence.

Producing this deliverable demonstrates the whole course: you can assess wireless and mobile systems, enforce and verify the controls, and present the evidence - safely and within scope.
