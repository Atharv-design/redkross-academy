For Personal/SAE networks, the passphrase IS the security: a weak passphrase can be recovered by offline guessing against a captured handshake (WPA2) or, with far more difficulty, attacked under WPA3-SAE (which resists offline guessing by design but still benefits from a strong passphrase). Guidance: use a long (15+ character), high-entropy, unique passphrase - ideally a random string or a long passphrase of several unrelated words - never a dictionary word, a date, or the company name. Rotate it if it may have leaked, and remember that in Personal mode everyone shares it (another reason to prefer Enterprise for corporate use). The lab flags a short/low-complexity passphrase (e.g. "himalaya2019") as HIGH and expects a long random one. Strong keys turn even a captured handshake into a dead end.
