Mobile testing must be authorised and safe:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| authorise | test only apps you own or are authorised to test. Decompiling, instrumenting or intercepting a third-party app without permission can breach terms, copyright and law. |
| controlled | do dynamic testing on a device/emulator YOU control, with a test build and test accounts, never against production users or data. |
| data care | apps and their traffic contain personal data; handle test data and any captured traffic responsibly and minimise it. |
| demo first | practise on an intentionally vulnerable DEMO app (as this course does) so no real app or user is involved. |

This course uses an original DEMO app precisely so all practice is on an artifact you own, reviewed statically and offline. In India, unauthorised access to a computer resource engages the IT Act, 2000 (ss.43, 66), and app data may be personal data under the DPDP Act, 2023; keep testing to authorised apps and controlled environments. This is not legal advice. The MASTG's methodology assumes authorised testing of your own or a client's app under agreement.
