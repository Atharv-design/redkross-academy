The course covered the wireless-and-mobile attack surface end to end, and the capstone integrates it:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| wireless edge | rogue/evil-twin detection (Modules 1, 3) and Wi-Fi hardening (Module 2) - securing the radio access. |
| Bluetooth/BLE | pairing, encryption and configuration (Module 4) - the short-range radio. |
| mobile app | the platform model and static review (Modules 5, 6) - insecure data handling and weakened defaults. |
| mobile API | authentication, transport and backend hardening (Module 7) - the untrusted-client backend. |

**The unifying principles:** assume the radio and network are hostile and encrypt; treat the client as untrusted and enforce server-side; verify controls with enforcing before/after evidence; and stay within an authorised, offline, do-no-harm scope. The capstone exercises all of it together.
