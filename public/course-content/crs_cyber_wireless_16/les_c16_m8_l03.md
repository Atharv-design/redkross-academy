An assessment is only useful if presented clearly:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| structure | scope and authorisation; method and tools; findings by area (wireless, Bluetooth, app, API) with severity; evidence (the before/after review outputs); remediation and verification. |
| evidence | the enforcing FAIL-to-PASS outputs and the findings lists are the evidence - reproducible, dated, tool-generated. |
| prioritise | HIGH findings and failed controls first, each with a concrete fix. |
| audience | a clear summary for decision-makers and the detail for engineers, as in Course 07/12 reporting. |

Clear presentation turns the technical work into decisions and fixes. The capstone deliverable is this presentation.
