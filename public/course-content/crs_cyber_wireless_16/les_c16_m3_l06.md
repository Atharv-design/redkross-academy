A wireless assessment produces evidence and a report:

- the configuration-review findings per AP (with severities and remediation), and the re-test showing fixes (FAIL -> PASS);
- the rogue/evil-twin detection results (impostor BSSIDs to investigate);
- a prioritised remediation list and an executive summary.

Because the configuration review and rogue detection are repeatable checks over data, their outputs are reproducible evidence - an auditor can re-run them. The report drives action: harden the failing configurations, investigate and remove the impostor APs, and re-assess. The lab's outputs (wifireview and roguescan results, saved in data/) are exactly this kind of evidence, and the capstone (Module 8) consolidates them. Good wireless reporting, like all assessment reporting, is specific, prioritised, evidence-backed and actionable.
