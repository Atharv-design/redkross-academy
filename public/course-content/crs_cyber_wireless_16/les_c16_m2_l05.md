A configuration review reads the AP/WLAN settings and flags each weakness against the controls above, with a severity. The reviewer checks: security mode (WPA3 preferred, WPA2-AES acceptable, no WEP/WPA/TKIP), cipher (AES-CCMP/GCMP, no TKIP/WEP), PMF (required), passphrase strength (for Personal/SAE), WPS (off), admin credentials (not default), firmware (current), and guest segmentation/isolation. The output is a prioritised findings list and an overall PASS/FAIL, re-runnable after hardening to confirm the fixes. This offline review is the defender's primary wireless assessment technique - it needs no radio activity and produces evidence. The lab's wifireview.py implements exactly this, and the deep dive walks its output on a weak and a hardened configuration.
