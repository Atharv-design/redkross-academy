The defender's goals over the air are confidentiality (traffic cannot be read), integrity/authenticity (frames and the AP cannot be forged), and availability (the network resists disruption) - achieved through encryption, PMF and robust design, plus monitoring for rogue/evil-twin APs. Crucially, wireless assessment is bounded by law and authorisation. Capturing, injecting, deauthenticating or connecting to networks you do not own is unlawful and unethical; radio use is regulated; and even a "test" that disrupts others is an offence. This course therefore keeps all practice OFFLINE: you review configurations, analyse provided scans, and harden your own settings - never transmitting, scanning or attacking. In India, unauthorised access to or interference with a computer resource engages the Information Technology Act, 2000 (ss.43, 66); lawful wireless assessment is confined to networks you own or are authorised to survey. This is not legal advice. The lab is built to keep you lawful by keeping you offline.
