Wireless assessment is powerful and must be bounded:

```text
authorisation   assess only networks you own or are authorised, in writing, to
                assess. Capturing, injecting or deauthenticating others'
                networks is unlawful.
non-disruption  do not deauthenticate clients, jam, or otherwise disrupt - even
                on your own network, and never on others'. Active techniques
                that disrupt are out of scope here.
radio regulation radio spectrum use is regulated; even transmitting can be an
                offence in the wrong context.
data handling   a scan may capture nearby networks and device identifiers;
                handle this data responsibly and minimise it.
```

This course therefore performs assessment from CONFIGURATIONS and PROVIDED SCANS - offline analysis that produces the same findings as a live survey without the assessor transmitting. Collecting a real scan, when done, is confined to your own /authorised networks with proper tools and never includes attacking or connecting to others. In India, unauthorised interception or access engages the IT Act, 2000 (ss.43, 66) and radio-use rules; this is not legal advice.
