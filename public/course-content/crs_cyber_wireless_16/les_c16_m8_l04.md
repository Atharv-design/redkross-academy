The course's scope rules apply throughout and are part of the capstone:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| authorised | test only your own or authorised networks, devices, apps and APIs, under written scope. |
| offline/own | all lab work is offline, against your own configurations, demo app and captured/synthetic data - no radio activity against others, no calls to third-party APIs, no access to systems you do not own. |
| do no harm | avoid disrupting networks, devices or services; agree limits for any testing that could affect availability. |
| data care | wireless, app and API data include personal data; handle responsibly and minimise. |
| law | in India, the IT Act, 2000 and DPDP Act, 2023 apply to access and personal data; stay within authorisation. This is not legal advice. |

The capstone is done entirely within this scope - the mark of a professional.
