The firewall and segmentation are the enforcing layers, and both can be verified offline. firewall.py lints a policy for the design flaws of Module 2 (no default-deny, any-any rules, shadowed rules, no egress filtering, overly broad ports). The lab's pre-hardening policy returns four findings (one HIGH); the hardened policy returns zero - verified. segmentation.py checks each connection against the zero-trust allowed-flow matrix of Module 4; the lab's connection set has three violations (user->db, internet->db, user->mgmt), each of which must be denied by policy and re-checked. Verification here means running the same check before and after and showing the finding count fall to zero - a repeatable, evidence-producing test, not a judgement call.
