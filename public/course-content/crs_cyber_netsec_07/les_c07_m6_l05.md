ROGUE-AP DETECTION finds unauthorised access points. Approaches: wireless scanning by the infrastructure (managed APs and wireless IDS sensors listen for SSIDs/BSSIDs not on the authorised list, and for an authorised SSID broadcast from an unexpected BSSID - a possible evil twin); correlating wireless observations with the wired side (an AP's MAC appearing on a switch port where no AP should be); and maintaining an authoritative inventory of approved APs so anything else is flagged. A Wireless Intrusion Detection/Prevention System (WIDS/WIPS) automates this and can alert on rogue and evil-twin APs, deauth floods and other wireless anomalies. The defensive principle mirrors the rest of the course: know what is authorised (an inventory/baseline), detect deviations, and investigate.
