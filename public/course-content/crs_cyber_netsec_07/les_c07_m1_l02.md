A TRUST ZONE is a group of systems with a similar trust level and purpose; a TRUST BOUNDARY is the line between zones, where traffic should be controlled and inspected. A typical enterprise has zones such as: INTERNET (untrusted); DMZ (internet-facing services like web servers - semi-trusted); APPLICATION (internal app tier); DATABASE (the most sensitive data); USER (employee workstations); and MANAGEMENT (administrative access to devices - highly sensitive). Traffic crossing a boundary - user to database, internet to DMZ - is where controls live. The key design rules: the database is reachable only from the application tier, never directly from users or the internet; management is reachable only from a controlled path; and the DMZ cannot freely initiate connections into the core. The lab models exactly these zones.
