The deliverable is a consolidated assessment that states, per layer: the control, its status (PASS/FAIL), the evidence (the tool output), and - where it failed - the gap and the remediation. The lab's review.py produces exactly this control summary, showing firewall, segmentation and TLS moving from FAIL to PASS with the finding counts as evidence, plus the telemetry signals for the monitoring layer. A good assessment is specific (named controls, counts, before/after), evidence-backed (every claim traceable to an output), prioritised (worst first), and actionable (each gap has a fix). It is the document that proves the network is defensible - and that is reproducible, because every check can be re-run.
