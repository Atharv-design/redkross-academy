Firewall policies change constantly, and uncontrolled changes are a top cause of outages and exposure. CHANGE CONTROL means every change is requested, reviewed (does it follow least privilege? does it shadow or contradict existing rules?), tested, approved, documented and logged, with a rollback plan. VALIDATION means checking the policy against standards before and after the change - ideally automatically, as a linter in a pipeline, so a change that removes the default-deny or adds an any-any rule is caught before it ships. This is "policy as code": the ruleset is a reviewed, version-controlled, tested artefact. The lab models it - firewall.py lint is the validation gate, and comparing the pre- and post-hardening policies is a reviewed change.
