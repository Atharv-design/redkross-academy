​## Task C07-CT1 On the offline lab you own, run firewall.py (on the pre-hardening and hardened policies) and segmentation.py. Produce the firewall findings before and after and the list of segmentation violations. For two findings, state the design principle violated (e.g. default-deny, zero trust) and the fix.

​## Task C07-CT2 Run tlscheck.py (pre-hardening and hardened), flowcheck.py and review.py. State the TLS findings before and after, the telemetry signals (scan, beacon, violations) with a containment action each, and the consolidated control summary (which controls moved FAIL->PASS and the evidence).
