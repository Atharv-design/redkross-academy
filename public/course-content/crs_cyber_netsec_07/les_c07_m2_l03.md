To know what a policy does to a given flow, you evaluate it: walk the rules in order and find the first match. This is how you verify intent ("can a user reach the database?") and find surprises. In the lab's pre-hardening policy, a user-to-database flow on 5432 is ALLOWED - not by a deliberate rule, but by an over-broad earlier rule "users may reach anything (1-65535)" that matches first. Evaluating the flow reveals this immediately. In the hardened policy, the same flow is explicitly DENIED by a specific rule, and an internet-to-database flow is denied by the final default-deny. Evaluation turns a wall of rules into answers about specific flows.
