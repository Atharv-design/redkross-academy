Even with the enforcing controls hardened, the monitoring layer (Module 7) checks for residual risk in actual traffic. flowcheck.py reviews the flow telemetry for scans, beaconing and segmentation violations. In the lab this surfaces a port scan, a beaconing host and three segmentation violations - signs that, beyond the static policy, real traffic needs investigation and containment. A complete assessment does not stop at "the policy is correct"; it confirms the telemetry is being watched and that the detections that should fire do fire. The telemetry review is what connects the static hardening to the living network.
