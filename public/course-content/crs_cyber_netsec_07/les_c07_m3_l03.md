ANOMALY-based detection builds a model of NORMAL behaviour and alerts on deviations - an unusual volume, a host contacting many ports, a connection at an odd hour, a new destination. Its strength: it can catch NOVEL activity that has no signature, including the scan and beaconing patterns in the lab. Its weakness: "abnormal" is not the same as "malicious", so anomaly detection tends to produce more false positives and needs a good baseline and careful thresholds. The two approaches are complementary: signatures for known threats, anomalies for the unknown. The lab's flowcheck.py (Module 7) is anomaly-style: it flags a host contacting many ports (scan) and a host beaconing at a constant interval, patterns defined by behaviour rather than a fixed signature.
