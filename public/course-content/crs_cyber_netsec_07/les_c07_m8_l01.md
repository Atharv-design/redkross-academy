A network security review walks every layer of defence-in-depth (Module 1) and asks, for each: is the control present, correctly configured, and verified? The layers from this course: perimeter and zones (DMZ, trust boundaries); firewall policy (default-deny, least privilege, egress filtering); IDS/IPS (detection tuned and monitored); segmentation (zero-trust flows enforced); secure connectivity (strong TLS/VPN); wireless (WPA2/3-Enterprise, no rogue APs); and monitoring/response (telemetry, detection, containment). The review produces a status per layer and a prioritised list of gaps. The lab gives a concrete version of this: a design, a firewall policy, a segmentation matrix, a TLS config and flow telemetry, each with a tool that returns PASS/FAIL. The review is the map; the rest of the module is verifying the hardening.
