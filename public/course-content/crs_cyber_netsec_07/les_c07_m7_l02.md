Network telemetry comes from routers and switches (NetFlow/IPFIX flow records), firewalls (allow/deny logs), DNS servers (query logs - a rich source for spotting malicious domains and beaconing), proxies (web access logs), and dedicated sensors. A flow record's key fields are: timestamp, source and destination IP, protocol, destination port, and byte/packet counts. From these alone a defender can see who talked to whom, when, how often and how much - enough to detect many attack patterns without reading any content. Good monitoring collects telemetry centrally (into a SIEM, Course 08), normalises it, and retains it long enough to investigate. The lab's telemetry (data/flows.jsonl) is a simplified NetFlow/IPFIX-style record set with exactly these fields.
