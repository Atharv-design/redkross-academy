ALERT TUNING is the ongoing work of making alerts actionable: disabling or tightening rules that fire on benign traffic; suppressing known-good sources (the vulnerability scanner, the monitoring system); setting thresholds so a single benign probe does not alert but a sustained pattern does; enriching alerts with context (asset criticality, reputation); and prioritising by severity and confidence. A well-tuned sensor produces few, high-value alerts that analysts trust and act on; an un-tuned one produces thousands that get ignored. Tuning is continuous because the environment and threats change. The lab illustrates the principle in its detectors: thresholds (for example, a scan requires many distinct ports within a time window; a beacon requires several contacts at a near-constant interval) exist precisely so that ordinary traffic does not trigger them - the normal, jittered web and app traffic in the telemetry is NOT flagged, while the scan and beacon are.
