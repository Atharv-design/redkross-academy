ISO/IEC 27001 requires top management to review the ISMS at planned intervals (clause 9.3). A practical agenda:

1. Status of actions from previous reviews.
2. Changes in context: business, threats, regulation (for example DPDP Rules commencement phases, SEBI or RBI updates, CA/Browser Forum certificate changes affecting operations).
3. Performance: metrics against targets, risk register summary and trends, incidents and near misses, audit results, exceptions, vendor status.
4. Feedback from interested parties: customers, regulators, staff.
5. Results of risk assessment and status of treatment plans.
6. Opportunities for improvement.
7. Decisions: risk acceptances above management authority, resource and budget, changes to the ISMS or policies.

**Outputs:** minutes with decisions, owners and dates, which serve as evidence for auditors, and an updated improvement plan. A good review takes 60-90 minutes, with papers circulated in advance, and ends with decisions rather than just "noted".
