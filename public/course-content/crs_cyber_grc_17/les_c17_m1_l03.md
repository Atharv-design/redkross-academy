The Institute of Internal Auditors' Three Lines Model describes roles rather than rigid departments:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| First line | management that owns and manages risk: IT operations, business units and security operations running controls. |
| Second line | expertise, support, monitoring and challenge: risk management, compliance and often the CISO's policy and oversight functions. |
| Third line | independent assurance: internal audit, reporting to the board's audit committee. |

External assurance (external auditors, certification bodies, regulators) sits outside the organisation. Independence matters. The people who operate a control should not be the only ones attesting that it works. Where the CISO both runs operations and reports on their effectiveness, add independent checks.
