Learners produce, for Tamarind Logistics (fictional) or an approved real organisation with anonymised data:

- a risk register of at least 12 risks with evidence-based residuals and treatments;
- a policy pack: the top-level policy, four topic policies with standards, two procedures, an exception register and a traceability matrix;
- a management review pack: agenda, metrics dashboard, executive summary and draft minutes with decisions.

Part C, section C4 holds the requirements and rubric.
