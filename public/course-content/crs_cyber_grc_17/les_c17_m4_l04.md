| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | the owner writes, using a template. |
| Review | subject-matter experts, legal and HR (for staff obligations), and the privacy lead (for personal data). |
| Approve | at the right level (Module 1 hierarchy). |
| Publish | in a central portal, with version control. |
| Communicate | through training and acknowledgement (attestation) for affected staff. |
| Implement | through controls, procedures and technical enforcement. |
| Monitor | compliance metrics and exceptions (L06). |
| Review | on schedule, after incidents, and when laws, frameworks or the business change. |
| Retire | archive superseded versions; keep them for audit history. |

ISO/IEC 27001 requires documented information to be controlled: approved, available where needed, protected and retained.
