## Revision

### Summary

Governance sets direction, risk appetite and accountability, and monitors. Management plans and runs, and operations performs. CSF 2.0 (February 2024) added the Govern function (GV.OC, GV.RM, GV.RR, GV.PO, GV.OV, GV.SC). Roles run from the board and committees through executives, CISO, CRO, CIO, DPO, legal and internal audit to asset owners and all staff. Sector regulators such as RBI (Master Direction, effective 1 April 2024) and SEBI (CSCRF, August 2024) add governance expectations. The three lines model separates running risk, challenge and independent assurance. A RACI has exactly one accountable person per activity, and business owners accept their risks. Policies state intent, standards mandate specifics, procedures describe steps and guidelines advise. All are owned, testable, reviewed and excepted formally. Board reports focus on risks against appetite, incidents, control health, assurance and decisions. The Uber concealment conviction shows why disclosure decisions need governance.

### Important concepts

accountability vs responsibility; independence; decision thresholds.

### Key technical takeaways

RACI construction; committee charter; policy hierarchy.

### Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Governance | Direction, accountability and oversight. |
| RACI | Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed. |
| Risk appetite | Amount of risk an organisation is willing to accept. |
| Standard | Mandatory specific requirement supporting a policy. |
| Three lines model | IIA model of risk roles and assurance. |

### Common mistakes

security accountable for all risk; policies nobody can test; no decision thresholds.
