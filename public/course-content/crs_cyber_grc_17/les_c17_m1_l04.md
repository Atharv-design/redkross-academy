| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Accountable | answers for the outcome and makes the final decision. There is exactly one accountable person per activity. |
| Responsible | does the work. There can be several. |
| Consulted | gives input before decisions. |
| Informed | is told afterwards. |

**Common governance failure:** "the security team" is accountable for everything. Security can advise and monitor, but business owners must be accountable for accepting risk to their assets and processes.
