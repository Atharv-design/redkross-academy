## Revision

### Summary

The capstone integrates GRC into three artefacts:

- a risk register built from assets, BIA, threats, control tests, exceptions, audit findings and vendor results;
- a policy pack of policies, standards, procedures, the exception register and a traceability matrix;
- a management review with an ISO 27001 clause 9.3-style agenda, ending in decisions with owners and dates.

Programme metrics cover risk, controls, compliance, resilience, third parties and response. Executive communication leads with decisions and options. The execution-tested toolkit outputs map directly to review decisions:

- R04 expired acceptance;
- CSF 15/22 coverage;
- invalid exceptions;
- the courier API RTO gap;
- four active leavers;
- QuickPrint's critical fails.

The CSRB's 2024 findings that Storm-0558 was preventable show why review recommendations must be tracked to completion.

### Important concepts

integration; decision-focused reviews; follow-through.

### Key technical takeaways

register inputs; traceability matrix; review agenda.

### Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Improvement plan | Prioritised roadmap from gaps and treatments. |
| Management review | Top-management evaluation of the ISMS. |
| Policy pack | Set of governance documents with traceability. |
| Traceability matrix | Mapping of requirements to controls, frameworks and risks. |

### Common mistakes

reviews that only note; disconnected artefacts; metrics without targets.
