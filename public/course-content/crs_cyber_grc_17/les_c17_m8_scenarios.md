## Scenario 1

The CEO asks for "the one number" that shows security posture.

**Task:** Respond.

> [!TIP] Model answer
> A single number hides what matters. Offer a one-page summary: residual risk against appetite (count of high risks beyond target), three key control indicators against target, and the decisions needed. Show trends.
>
> **Lesson:** concise but honest reporting.

## Scenario 2

The review has 40 agenda items and runs 3 hours without decisions.

**Task:** Redesign.

> [!TIP] Model answer
> Circulate papers in advance. Limit the meeting to decision items and exceptions to targets, with "for information" items in the pack only. Timebox each item, end each with a decision, and track actions.
>
> **Lesson:** reviews exist to decide.

## Scenario 3

An external review's recommendations were accepted last year but none are complete.

**Task:** Address in the management review.

> [!TIP] Model answer
> Present the status of each recommendation with owner, blockers and revised dates. Escalate resource needs and ask for decisions to fund, re-prioritise or accept the risk formally. Report progress to the board, and treat repeat delays as a governance finding.
>
> **Lesson:** follow-through is governance.
