## About this module

Organisations rely on vendors for software, cloud services, payroll, logistics, support and much more. Each relationship can extend your attack surface: vendors hold your data, connect to your network, run code inside your environment, or provide services you cannot operate without. Third-party risk management (TPRM) identifies and tiers suppliers, assesses them before onboarding, writes security into contracts, monitors them over the relationship, and plans for exit. This module covers each stage. The lab toolkit scores two vendor questionnaires, including "critical fail" items that override a good total score.

## Why it matters

In 2023, the CL0P group exploited a vulnerability in the MOVEit Transfer file-transfer product. Many organisations were affected through the product they ran or through service providers who ran it on their behalf (case study). CSF 2.0 made supply chain risk management a Govern category (GV.SC), and Indian financial regulators also expect vendor risk assessment.

## Where it is used

Procurement and vendor management offices, security and privacy teams, legal, outsourcing governance in regulated entities, and customer assurance programmes (where you are the vendor).

## Learning objectives

1. Inventory and tier third parties by inherent risk.
2. Design proportionate due diligence: questionnaires, evidence, assessments.
3. Interpret certifications and reports, including their scope.
4. Write contractual security, privacy and incident obligations.
5. Monitor vendors and respond to vendor incidents.
6. Manage concentration risk and exit.
