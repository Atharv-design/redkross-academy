Build a vendor inventory with:

- owner (business sponsor);
- service description;
- data types and volumes;
- access type;
- criticality to processes (BIA, Module 5);
- location of data and processing;
- contract dates.

Tier by inherent risk, before considering the vendor's controls:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 critical | sensitive data at scale, privileged access, or a critical-process dependency. Full assessment, annual review, strongest contract terms. |
| Tier 2 high | confidential data or limited access. Standard assessment, review every 1-2 years. |
| Tier 3 low | no sensitive data or access. Light-touch checks. |

Tiering keeps effort proportionate. Not every stationery supplier needs a 200-question survey.
