**Principles:**

- Lead with the decision needed and its business impact.
- Use few numbers, with trend and target. Avoid jargon.
- Present options: for example, "Option A: automate leaver deprovisioning, INR X, 8 weeks, reduces R03 residual to 6. Option B: accept, with weekly manual reconciliation as an interim control."
- Be honest about uncertainty and residual risk.
- Link to obligations: CERT-In, DPDP, sector regulators, customer contracts.
- Follow up with decisions recorded and actions tracked.

A one-page executive summary should state:

- overall posture against appetite;
- the top 5 risks with trends;
- incidents of note;
- assurance highlights;
- decisions requested.
