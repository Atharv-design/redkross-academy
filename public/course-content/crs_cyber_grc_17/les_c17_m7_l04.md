Key clauses (draft with legal):

- security requirements: a defined baseline or reference to your standards, encryption, MFA, vulnerability management, logging;
- data protection: purpose limitation, processing only on instructions, location and transfer conditions, deletion or return at end, and sub-processor approval (DPDP Data Processor obligations);
- incident notification: a defined timeframe (for example 24 hours or less, so you can meet CERT-In's 6 hours and DPDP intimation duties), content and cooperation;
- audit and assurance rights: reports, questionnaires and audits for critical vendors;
- service levels and continuity: availability, RTO and RPO matching your BIA (Module 5), BCP testing evidence;
- liability, indemnity and insurance;
- termination and exit: data return format, transition assistance, escrow for critical software.

Contracts are controls. Track them in the vendor inventory and test them, for example by checking that notification clauses work in tabletop exercises.
