Procedures describe how a team performs a task reliably:

- trigger and preconditions (for example "new joiner ticket approved by manager");
- numbered steps with the tool and screen or command;
- decision points and escalation contacts;
- records produced, which become audit evidence (Module 6);
- timing and SLA.

They are owned by the operating team, kept current with tooling changes, and tested in practice (walkthroughs, tabletop exercises for incident procedures). Runbooks and playbooks (Course 08 Module 7, Course 09 Module 7) are procedures for operations and incident response.
