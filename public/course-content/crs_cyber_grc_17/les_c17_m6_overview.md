## About this module

**Assurance answers one question:** do our controls actually work? Policies and mappings describe intent, and assurance tests reality. This module covers:

- planning an audit or assessment;
- collecting reliable evidence;
- testing control design and operating effectiveness;
- sampling, and when full-population testing is better;
- writing findings that management can act on;
- tracking remediation to closure.

The lab toolkit draws a reproducible audit sample and compares it with a full-population test. The sample missed all four exceptions the full test found.

## Why it matters

Controls fail silently. At Equifax in 2017, according to the US GAO, a monitoring device could not inspect encrypted traffic because a digital certificate had expired about 10 months earlier, and the intrusion was detected only after it was renewed (case study). A routine control test would have asked whether the monitoring was working.

## Where it is used

Internal audit, external audits (ISO 27001 certification, SOC 2-type attestations, financial audits with IT general controls), regulator inspections (RBI and SEBI audits of regulated entities), customer audits, and self-assessments by the second line.

## Learning objectives

1. Plan an audit: scope, criteria, risk focus and approach.
2. Collect sufficient, reliable and relevant evidence.
3. Test control design and operating effectiveness.
4. Use sampling correctly, and use data analytics for full-population testing.
5. Write findings with condition, criteria, cause, effect and recommendation.
6. Manage remediation and report assurance results.
