**Steps:**

```text
1. Identify business processes and their owners, such as dispatch and
   tracking, customer portal and payroll.
2. Assess impacts of disruption over time (1 hour, 4 hours, 1 day, 1
   week) across financial, customer, regulatory and legal, safety and
   reputational dimensions (the scales from Module 2).
3. Determine:
     MTD  maximum tolerable downtime, beyond which damage is
          unacceptable or irrecoverable;
     RTO  recovery time objective, the target time to restore the
          process. It must be less than or equal to MTD;
     RPO  recovery point objective, the maximum acceptable data loss,
          measured in time.
4. Identify dependencies: applications, data, infrastructure, people,
   facilities, suppliers.
5. Identify minimum resources and workarounds, such as manual dispatch
   sheets.
```

Interview process owners, not only IT. Validate figures with finance and leadership, because an RTO is a business decision with a cost.
