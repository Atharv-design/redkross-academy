ISO/IEC 27001 specifies requirements for an information security management system (ISMS). The requirement clauses, 4 to 10, follow the harmonised structure used by ISO management-system standards:

```text
4 Context of the organisation (scope, interested parties)
5 Leadership (commitment, policy, roles)
6 Planning (risk assessment and treatment, objectives)
7 Support (resources, competence, awareness, communication,
  documented information)
8 Operation (performing risk assessment and treatment)
9 Performance evaluation (monitoring, internal audit, management review)
10 Improvement (nonconformity, corrective action, continual improvement)
```

Annex A lists reference controls. In the 2022 edition, published summaries report 93 controls in four themes (organisational 37, people 8, physical 14, technological 34), restructured from 114 controls in the 2013 edition, with 11 new controls. The transition period for certified organisations ended on 31 October 2025 [R2]. The standard is a paid, copyrighted document and is not reproduced here. Verify control details against your licensed copy (SME review flag). The Statement of Applicability (SoA) lists each Annex A control, whether it is applicable, the justification, and its implementation status. Risk treatment decisions drive control selection, not the other way round. Certification is by accredited certification bodies, through stage 1 and stage 2 audits and then surveillance audits. ISO/IEC 27002 gives guidance on implementing the controls.
