**Good evidence is:**

- sufficient (enough of it);
- reliable (from independent or system sources rather than verbal assurance);
- relevant (it addresses the criteria);
- timely (it covers the audit period).

Reliability, from strongest to weakest:

1. evidence the auditor obtains directly, such as system-generated reports run by the auditor or re-performance;
2. system records provided by the auditee, such as logs, tickets and configuration exports;
3. documents prepared by the auditee, such as spreadsheets and attestations;
4. verbal explanations, which need corroboration.

**Handle evidence carefully:**

- record source, date, who provided it and how it was obtained;
- protect any personal data in samples (DPDP);
- retain it per audit policy;
- be able to show an evidence trail for each finding (Course 09 principles of integrity apply).
