## Revision

### Summary

Frameworks guide, standards certify, regulations bind and guidance advises. Choose a primary framework and map the rest. NIST CSF 2.0 (February 2024) has a Core of 6 Functions and 22 Categories (GV.OC through RC.CO), Profiles (Current and Target) and Tiers (Partial, Risk Informed, Repeatable, Adaptive). ISO/IEC 27001:2022 specifies an ISMS through clauses 4-10, with Annex A reference controls (published summaries report 93 in four themes) selected through risk treatment and recorded in the SoA. The transition ended on 31 October 2025. Indian obligations include CERT-In Directions, the DPDP Act and Rules, RBI's IT governance Master Direction and SEBI's CSCRF (August 2024). A unified control set mapped many-to-many reduces duplicate effort. The tested toolkit found 15/22 CSF categories covered and listed the gaps. Coverage is not effectiveness, and certification has scope and time limits.

### Important concepts

primary framework plus mappings; SoA; profiles.

### Key technical takeaways

UCS record fields; gap analysis; mapping rationale.

### Common mistakes

treating certification as security; mapping without rationale; parallel compliance projects.
