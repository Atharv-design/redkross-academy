Boards need decision-useful information, not technical dashboards:

- Top risks against appetite: trend, owner, treatment status and dates.
- Material incidents and near misses: impact, lessons and regulatory notifications made (CERT-In, DPDP Board, sector regulators).
- Control health: a few key indicators, such as critical patching within SLA, MFA coverage, backup restore success and phishing report rates, with targets.
- Programme delivery: milestones and funding.
- Assurance results: audit findings, open and overdue.
- Decisions requested: risk acceptances above management authority, and investment choices.

Present choices with options and consequences ("fund X to reduce risk R03 from 16 to 6 by Q2, or formally accept"). Avoid fear, uncertainty and doubt. Regulators increasingly expect board-level oversight of cyber risk. Indian financial regulators' frameworks (RBI directions, SEBI's Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework of 20 August 2024) set governance expectations for regulated entities (Module 3).
