You cannot protect, or assess risk to, what you do not know you have.

**Inventory:**

- information: customer data, payroll, intellectual property;
- systems and applications, infrastructure and cloud resources;
- endpoints and devices, including OT and IoT;
- third-party services (Module 7);
- people and key roles.

For each, record:

- owner (accountable), custodian (operates it) and location (including cloud region and data residency);
- classification (for example public, internal, confidential, restricted), with personal data flagged for DPDP purposes;
- criticality to business processes (links to the BIA, Module 5);
- dependencies and interfaces.

Keep inventories current through automation: CMDB synchronisation, cloud APIs, EDR and network discovery, and SaaS discovery. CSF 2.0's ID.AM category covers asset management [R2].
