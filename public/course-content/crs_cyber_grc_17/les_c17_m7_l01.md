**Types of exposure:**

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Data | the vendor stores or processes your data (SaaS, payroll, CRM). |
| Access | the vendor connects to your systems (MSPs, support, remote-access tools). |
| Software | the vendor's code runs in your environment (agents, libraries, on-premises products). |
| Service | you depend on the vendor's availability (cloud, logistics APIs, payment gateways). |
| Fourth parties | your vendors' vendors (sub-processors, hosting providers). |

Privacy law matters. Under India's DPDP framework, a Data Fiduciary remains responsible for personal data processed by its Data Processors, so contracts and oversight must reflect that (Course 01 Module 7). RBI's IT governance Master Direction requires vendor risk assessment for third-party IT arrangements, according to published summaries (Module 1).
