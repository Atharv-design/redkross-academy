## Scenario 1

A manager insists the control works because "we've never had a problem".

**Task:** Respond as auditor.

> [!TIP] Model answer
> Absence of known incidents is not evidence that a control works, because undetected failures are possible (the Equifax monitoring gap). Request system evidence of operation over the period, test instances or the full population, and report on the evidence.
>
> **Lesson:** evidence, not anecdote.

## Scenario 2

Evidence provided is a spreadsheet the auditee created.

**Task:** Assess reliability.

> [!TIP] Model answer
> It is lower reliability. Ask for the source system extract, or observe it being run. Reconcile totals to the system, and test a sample of rows back to the source. Document the reliability assessment.
>
> **Lesson:** go to the source.

## Scenario 3

Management wants to close a finding before the fix is complete.

**Task:** Respond.

> [!TIP] Model answer
> Keep it open with a revised date or an interim compensating control. Close only after a re-test shows the fix works (for example, a full reconciliation shows zero leavers active). Report the status honestly to the committee.
>
> **Lesson:** closure requires validation.
