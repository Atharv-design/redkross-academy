| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Governance | sets direction (strategy, risk appetite, policy), assigns accountability and monitors performance. Board and executive level. |
| Management | plans, builds, runs and monitors security to meet that direction. CISO and IT leadership. |
| Operations | performs the work: patching, monitoring, access reviews. |

NIST CSF 2.0 (26 February 2024) added a Govern function (GV) alongside Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond and Recover. Its categories are Organizational Context, Risk Management Strategy, Roles, Responsibilities and Authorities, Policy, Oversight, and Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management [R1]. That change signals that governance is part of cybersecurity, not a separate paperwork exercise.

**Good governance produces:**

- clear objectives tied to business goals;
- a stated risk appetite;
- named accountable owners;
- policies people follow;
- honest metrics;
- escalation that works when things go wrong.
