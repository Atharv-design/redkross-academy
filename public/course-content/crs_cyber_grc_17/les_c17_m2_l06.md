**Register fields:**

- ID and scenario;
- asset and owner;
- inherent likelihood, impact and score;
- existing controls and their evidence;
- residual likelihood, impact and score;
- treatment, action plan and due dates;
- acceptance details;
- trend and last review.

A risk appetite statement translates the board's tolerance into criteria, for example:

```text
"No tolerance for residual risks scoring 16+ affecting customer personal
data; residual 12-15 requires executive acceptance and a treatment plan
within 90 days; critical vulnerabilities on internet-facing systems
remediated within 14 days."
```

Key risk indicators (KRIs) warn of rising risk: overdue critical patches, MFA coverage below target, failed restore tests, phishing click rates. Review the register monthly at management level and quarterly at the committee, and update it after incidents and major changes.
