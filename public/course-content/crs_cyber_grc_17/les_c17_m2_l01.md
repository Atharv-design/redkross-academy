Most frameworks describe the same loop:

1. Establish context: scope, objectives, criteria and appetite.
2. Identify assets, threats, vulnerabilities and scenarios.
3. Analyse likelihood and impact.
4. Evaluate against appetite and prioritise.
5. Treat.
6. Monitor and review.
7. Communicate throughout.

NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1, "Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments" (September 2012), describes preparing for, conducting, communicating and maintaining risk assessments [R1]. ISO/IEC 27005 gives guidance for information security risk management aligned with ISO/IEC 27001, and ISO 31000 covers enterprise risk management generally. CSF 2.0 places risk strategy in GV.RM and assessment in ID.RA (Module 3). Use one consistent method across the organisation so risks can be compared and aggregated.
