A balanced set of 8-12 metrics with targets:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Risk | high residual risks open, and those beyond target date. |
| Controls | MFA coverage, critical patch SLA compliance, backup restore test success, leaver deprovisioning within 1 day (the lab finding). |
| Compliance | policy acknowledgement rate, expired exceptions (target 0), overdue audit findings. |
| Resilience | critical processes with tested recovery meeting RTO (Module 5). |
| Third party | Tier 1 vendors assessed in the last 12 months; vendors with open critical fails. |
| Response | mean time to detect and contain (Course 08 metrics); regulatory notifications made on time. |

**Improvement plan:** a prioritised 12-month roadmap from the CSF Target Profile gaps (Module 3) and risk treatments (Module 2), with owners and budget. Review it quarterly.
