## Scenario 1

A vendor claims "ISO 27001 certified" in a sales deck.

**Task:** Verify what that means.

> [!TIP] Model answer
> Request the certificate. Check the certification body and its accreditation, the scope (sites, services), the validity dates and the standard version (2022; 2013 certificates are no longer valid after the transition). Ask for the SoA summary and the latest surveillance audit outcome. Confirm the scope covers the service you are buying.
>
> **Lesson:** certificates have scopes and dates.

## Scenario 2

Leadership wants to "get compliant" with five frameworks in six months.

**Task:** Propose an approach.

> [!TIP] Model answer
> Choose a primary framework. Build a unified control set from risk priorities. Map the five frameworks to it, prioritise mandatory regulations first, and identify overlaps and true gaps. Plan phased implementation with evidence design. Set realistic certification timelines and communicate them.
>
> **Lesson:** map once, implement once.

## Scenario 3

The mapping shows 22/22 CSF coverage, but an incident still succeeded.

**Task:** Explain.

> [!TIP] Model answer
> Mapping shows that a control exists for each category, not that it is effective, configured correctly or covers all assets. Test effectiveness (Module 6), use threat-informed validation, review the control's scope, and update the risk register with the incident lessons.
>
> **Lesson:** coverage is not effectiveness.
