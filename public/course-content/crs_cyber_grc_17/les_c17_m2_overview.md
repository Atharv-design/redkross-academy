## About this module

Risk management is how an organisation decides where to spend limited security effort. It identifies what matters (assets), what could go wrong (threat scenarios), how likely and how harmful that would be, what controls already reduce it, and what to do about what remains: mitigate, transfer, avoid or accept. It is a continuous cycle, not an annual form. This module covers asset inventories, risk identification, qualitative and quantitative analysis, treatment, documented acceptance, and the risk register as a living management tool. The lab toolkit computes inherent and residual risk and catches an expired acceptance.

## Why it matters

Regulators judge risk management, not just incidents. In August 2020 the US bank regulator OCC assessed an USD 80 million penalty against Capital One, citing the bank's failure to establish effective risk assessment processes before migrating significant IT operations to the public cloud (case study).

## Where it is used

Enterprise risk management, security programme planning, project and change approvals, vendor onboarding (Module 7), regulatory reporting and insurance.

## Learning objectives

1. Build and maintain an asset inventory with owners and classifications.
2. Write risk scenarios that link asset, threat, vulnerability and impact.
3. Score risks qualitatively and understand quantitative approaches.
4. Distinguish inherent from residual risk and evaluate control effect.
5. Choose and document treatments, including formal acceptance with expiry.
6. Operate a risk register and report against risk appetite.
