| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| People | cross-training, succession for key roles, remote-work capability, contact trees. |
| Process | manual workarounds (paper dispatch, offline price lists), reduced-service modes, prioritised customers. |
| Sites | alternate offices or depots, work-from-home, reciprocal arrangements. |
| Technology | redundancy (multi-AZ or multi-region, Course 11), failover, alternative providers. |
| Suppliers | dual sourcing, contractual SLAs and continuity obligations, escrow (Module 7). |
| Communications | pre-approved templates for customers, staff, regulators and media. These matter for regulated notifications such as CERT-In within 6 hours (Course 01 Module 7). |
