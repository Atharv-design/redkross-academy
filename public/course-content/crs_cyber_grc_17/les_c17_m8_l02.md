**Contents:**

1. Information Security Policy (top-level, board-approved).
2. Topic policies, for example acceptable use, access control, data protection and classification, incident response, business continuity, supplier security, secure development (Course 12) and cryptography (Course 15).
3. Standards for each topic, with testable requirements (Module 4).
4. Key procedures: joiner/mover/leaver, incident escalation (CERT-In 6 hours), restore testing, vendor onboarding.
5. The exception register and process.
6. A traceability matrix linking requirements to CSF categories, ISO 27001 controls, regulatory clauses, controls (UCS) and risks.
7. Document control: owners, versions, approvals, review dates.

Keep each document short, and publish the pack in a searchable, version-controlled portal. The matrix lets a reviewer confirm, for example, that every CERT-In obligation is covered by a standard and a procedure.
