## Scenario 1

A business head says "just accept all the risks, we have insurance".

**Task:** Respond.

> [!TIP] Model answer
> Insurance transfers part of the financial loss, with exclusions and conditions that often require controls such as MFA and backups. It does not cover regulatory obligations, operational disruption, customer trust or personal liability. Acceptance must be within appetite, by the accountable owner, and documented with expiry. Propose prioritised mitigation for the high risks.
>
> **Lesson:** insurance complements controls.

## Scenario 2

The register has 300 risks, most stale.

**Task:** Restore its usefulness.

> [!TIP] Model answer
> Consolidate duplicates into scenario-level risks. Archive closed ones. Require an owner and a review date for each. Re-score the top 30 with current evidence, and set a cadence (monthly for high risks, quarterly for others). Link risks to controls and KRIs, and report the top 10 to the committee.
>
> **Lesson:** a register is a management tool, not an archive.

## Scenario 3

A cloud migration starts next month without a risk assessment.

**Task:** Advise.

> [!TIP] Model answer
> Pause or condition the migration on a risk assessment covering the shared responsibility model, identity, network exposure, data protection, logging and incident response (Course 11). Define go-live criteria and owners, and cite the OCC Capital One finding as a regulatory example.
>
> **Lesson:** assess before change.
