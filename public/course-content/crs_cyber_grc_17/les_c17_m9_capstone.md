​### Objectives

Demonstrate LO1-LO8 by building the core GRC artefacts for an organisation and driving decisions.

​### Scenario

Tamarind Logistics (fictional; INR 800 crore revenue; 1,200 staff; depots across India; customer portal and courier integrations; personal data of customers and staff) has appointed you as its first Head of GRC. The board wants a management review in eight weeks.

​### Requirements

```text
R1 Governance: committee charter with decision thresholds; RACI for 10
   activities (M01).
R2 Risk register: at least 12 risks, asset-linked, with inherent and
   evidence-based residual scores, treatments, owners, and acceptances
   with expiry; appetite statement and 4 KRIs (M02).
R3 Unified control set of at least 15 controls mapped to all 22 CSF 2.0
   categories and to applicable Indian obligations (flag for SME
   review) (M03).
R4 Policy pack: top-level policy, four topic policies with standards,
   two procedures, exception register with at least three entries and
   traceability matrix (M04).
R5 BIA for three processes with dependency checks and one tested
   recovery runbook outline (M05).
R6 One audit (plan, tests including a full-population test, two
   findings) (M06).
R7 Vendor assessment of three vendors with tiering and critical items
   (M07).
R8 Management review pack: agenda, metrics with targets, one-page
   executive summary, decisions requested, and minutes from a role-play
   (M08).
```

​### Deliverables

Register (spreadsheet or toolkit extension), policy pack documents, UCS and traceability matrix, BIA, audit report, vendor assessments, management review pack and minutes; a 1-page reflection on limitations.
