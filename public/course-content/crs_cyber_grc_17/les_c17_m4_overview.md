## About this module

Policies, standards and procedures are how governance decisions reach everyday work. Well-written documents are short, clear, owned, testable and followed. Badly written ones are long, vague, contradictory and ignored, or followed in ways that create risk. This module teaches how to draft each document type, how to write requirements that can be tested, how to manage the document lifecycle, and how to run an exception process that keeps unavoidable deviations visible, time-limited and compensated. The lab toolkit checks an exception register for expiry, missing compensating controls and inadequate approval.

## Why it matters

Every organisation has exceptions. Some systems cannot meet a standard yet. The danger is when exceptions become invisible or permanent, or their risk is under-appraised. The British Library's review of its 2023 ransomware attack records that MFA had been deliberately scoped out of domain access, including the terminal server later identified as the likely entry point (case study).

## Where it is used

Policy management in every organisation; ISO 27001 documented information; regulator inspections, which ask for policies and evidence they are applied; and customer due diligence.

## Learning objectives

1. Draft a policy, a standard and a procedure for the same topic.
2. Write requirements that are specific, testable and owned.
3. Manage policy lifecycle: approval, publication, acknowledgement and review.
4. Design and run an exception (waiver) process.
5. Link exceptions to the risk register and compensating controls.
6. Measure policy compliance.
