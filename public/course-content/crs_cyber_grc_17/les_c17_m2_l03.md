A good risk statement links cause, event and consequence for a specific asset: "Because partner logins accept passwords only (vulnerability), credential stuffing (threat event) could let attackers access partner shipment data (asset), causing data exposure, regulatory notification and loss of a major partner (impact)."

**Sources of scenarios:**

- threat intelligence (Courses 08, 14);
- incidents and near misses (yours and your sector's);
- audit findings (Module 6);
- vulnerability data;
- threat models of new systems (Course 12 Module 2);
- business changes such as a new cloud migration, an acquisition or a new regulation;
- interviews with process owners.

Avoid vague risks ("cyber attack") and control-absence statements ("no SIEM") that hide the actual consequence.
