## Scenario 1

A critical SaaS vendor refuses audit rights but offers an ISO 27001 certificate.

**Task:** Decide.

> [!TIP] Model answer
> Check the certificate's scope, validity and certification body, and ask for the SoA and any independent assurance report covering the service. Negotiate alternative assurance rights (questionnaires, reports, a right to audit after an incident). Assess the residual risk and have it accepted at the right level, or choose another vendor.
>
> **Lesson:** certificates help but have scopes.

## Scenario 2

CISA and CERT-In publish an advisory for a product you think a vendor uses.

**Task:** Act.

> [!TIP] Model answer
> Query the vendor inventory and contact vendors with a short, targeted questionnaire (use, version, patch status, exposure, indicators). Invoke the notification clauses. Assess your own data exposure and notification duties. Track responses and escalate non-responses.
>
> **Lesson:** prepared processes make advisory response fast.

## Scenario 3

Procurement wants to remove security review to speed up purchasing.

**Task:** Propose a compromise.

> [!TIP] Model answer
> Tier at intake with 5 questions. Tier 3 gets automatic approval with standard terms. Tier 2 gets a short questionnaire within agreed SLAs. Tier 1 gets a full review. Pre-approve common vendors, and measure cycle times.
>
> **Lesson:** proportionate review keeps business moving.
