## SEBI CSCRF, 2024 - consolidating cyber obligations -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Context: On 20 August 2024, the Securities and Exchange Board of India issued the Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework (CSCRF) for SEBI Regulated Entities, circular SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_CSC_EXT/P/CIR/2024/113. Published summaries describe it as consolidating earlier SEBI cyber circulars and applying a graded approach. Regulated entities are classified into categories (market infrastructure institutions, qualified, mid-size, small-size and self-certification regulated entities) based on criteria such as clients, trade volume and assets. Later circulars clarified the requirements and extended some timelines [C17-M03-R3]. Lessons: - Regulators are consolidating and structuring cyber requirements, often in framework-like form. A unified control set with mappings makes adoption faster. - Proportionality: obligations scale with the entity's category. Know your classification. - Track clarifications and timeline changes. Compliance calendars and regulatory-change monitoring are part of GRC. Verification note: details are from secondary legal and advisory summaries found by web search. The primary circular was not opened in this build. Compliance SME review is required before use.
