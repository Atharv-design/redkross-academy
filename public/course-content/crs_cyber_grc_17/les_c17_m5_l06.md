Test types, in increasing realism:

- plan review;
- walkthrough;
- tabletop exercise (scenario discussion);
- simulation;
- component recovery test (restore a database);
- full failover or recovery test;
- unannounced exercise.

Test at least annually for critical processes, and after major changes. Record outcomes against RTO and RPO, findings, and owners for fixes.

**Governance:**

- a BC policy with scope and roles;
- annual BIA refresh;
- plan owners;
- a test schedule and results reported to the committee;
- KRIs: last successful restore test, percentage of critical systems meeting RTO in tests, backup immutability coverage;
- integration with incident response and crisis management.
