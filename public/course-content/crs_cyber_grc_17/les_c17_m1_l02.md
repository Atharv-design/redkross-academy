```text
Board of directors  ultimate accountability. Approves risk appetite and
                    key policies, oversees major risks and requires
                    reporting. Often acts through a risk or audit
                    committee.
CEO and executives  own business risks, including cyber risk in their
                    areas, and fund and prioritise.
CISO                leads the security programme. Advises on risk,
                    sets standards, monitors and reports. The CISO
                    should be able to escalate to the board or a
                    committee.
CRO / risk function enterprise risk framework; challenges and
                    aggregates risks.
CIO / CTO           runs technology and implements many controls.
DPO / privacy lead  personal-data obligations (DPDP Act and Rules,
                    sector rules).
Legal and compliance  regulatory interpretation, notification decisions
                    (CERT-In 6-hour reporting, DPDP breach intimation;
                    Course 01 Module 7).
Internal audit      independent assurance to the board (Module 6).
Asset, system and data owners  accountable for their assets' risks;
                    approve access and accept residual risks within
                    delegated limits.
All staff           follow policy and report incidents.
```
