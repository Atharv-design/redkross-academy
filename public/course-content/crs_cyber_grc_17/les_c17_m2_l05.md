**Treatment options:**

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Mitigate | add or improve controls; the most common option. |
| Transfer | insurance or contractual allocation. Liability can transfer; accountability does not. |
| Avoid | stop the activity (decommission the system, do not launch the feature). |
| Accept | tolerate the residual risk within appetite. |

**Acceptance must be:**

- made by the accountable owner at the right authority level (committee thresholds, Module 1);
- documented with rationale, compensating controls and review or expiry date;
- visible in the register and reported.

The toolkit flags expired acceptances and any high residual risk not being mitigated. Treatment plans need actions, owners, dates and budget, tracked to completion.
