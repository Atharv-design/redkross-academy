| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Frameworks | outcome-oriented and flexible (NIST CSF, CIS Controls, COBIT for IT governance). |
| Standards | formal and certifiable (ISO/IEC 27001; PCI DSS for payment cards). |
| Regulations | legally binding (DPDP Act and Rules, CERT-In Directions, sector regulators' directions). |
| Guidance | technical recommendations (NIST SP 800 series, CERT-In advisories, vendor benchmarks). |

Choose a primary framework for structure, often CSF or ISO 27001, and map the others to it. Tell stakeholders which is which: failing a regulation has different consequences from not following a voluntary framework.
