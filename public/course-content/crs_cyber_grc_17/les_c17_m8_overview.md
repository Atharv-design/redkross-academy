## About this module

The capstone brings the course together into the three artefacts a security governance function produces every year:

- a risk register with treatment plans;
- a policy pack of policies, standards, procedures and the exception register;
- a management review that tells leadership how the programme is performing and what decisions are needed.

This module explains how to assemble them from the work in Modules 1-7, how to run a management review meeting, and how to measure and improve the GRC programme. It ends with the capstone brief and rubric.

## Why it matters

Leaders, auditors and regulators judge security governance by these artefacts and by whether they drive action. The US Cyber Safety Review Board's 2024 report on the 2023 Exchange Online intrusion described it as preventable and the result of a cascade of failures, and it made recommendations aimed at governance and culture as well as technology (case study).

## Where it is used

Annual ISMS management reviews (ISO/IEC 27001 clause 9.3), board risk committee reporting, regulatory submissions, and programme planning and budgeting cycles.

## Learning objectives

1. Consolidate risks, controls, exceptions, findings and vendor results into a coherent register.
2. Assemble a policy pack with traceability to frameworks and risks.
3. Prepare and run a management review with decisions and actions.
4. Define GRC programme metrics and an improvement plan.
5. Communicate residual risk and asks to executives.
6. Complete the capstone deliverables.

## Before you start

**Prerequisites:** C17-M01 to C17-M07.

**Estimated study time:** About 7 hours (plus 14 hours capstone).
