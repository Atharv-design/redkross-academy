| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Self-assessment | the first line attests to its own controls. Useful, but not independent. |
| Second-line review | risk or compliance monitoring (Module 1, three lines). |
| Internal audit | independent assurance to the audit committee. |
| External audit/certification | ISO 27001 certification audits, attestation reports, and IT general controls in financial audits. |
| Regulatory inspection | sector regulators reviewing compliance with their directions. |
| Technical assurance | vulnerability assessment, penetration testing and red teaming (Courses 05, 19). These test resistance to attack rather than conformance. |

Combine them so they complement one another without duplicating effort. A single evidence repository per control (Module 3 UCS) serves all of them.
