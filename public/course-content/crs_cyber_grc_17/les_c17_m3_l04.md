A summary for orientation (confirm the current text and applicability with compliance; Course 01 Module 7 has details):

- IT Act, 2000 and rules; CERT-In Directions of 28 April 2022: 6-hour incident reporting, 180-day log retention in India, clock synchronisation, and designated points of contact.
- DPDP Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025 (notified 13 November 2025, phased commencement): obligations for Data Fiduciaries including security safeguards and breach intimation (Rule 7, 18-month tranche).
- RBI: Master Direction on IT Governance, Risk, Controls and Assurance Practices (7 November 2023, effective 1 April 2024) and other directions for regulated entities.
- SEBI: Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework (CSCRF) for SEBI Regulated Entities (circular of 20 August 2024), which consolidated earlier cyber circulars and grades obligations by entity category (case study).
- Others by sector: IRDAI (insurance), telecoms regulations, and government guidelines for ministries and critical information infrastructure (NCIIPC).

Regulations frequently reference frameworks or use their structure, which helps mapping.
