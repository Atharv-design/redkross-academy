**Measures:**

- policy acknowledgement rate;
- technical compliance rates, such as MFA coverage, patch SLA compliance and encryption coverage;
- number of exceptions by age, risk and expiry status;
- audit findings per policy area;
- incidents linked to policy breaches.

Prefer automated measurement (configuration scans, identity reports, cloud posture tools; Course 11) over self-assessment. Report trends against targets to management and the board (Module 1). Investigate low compliance before blaming people: unclear standards, missing tools or unrealistic procedures are common causes.
