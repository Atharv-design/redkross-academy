- Periodic reassessment by tier, plus triggers: a contract renewal, a scope change, a vendor breach, an acquisition, or a new regulation.
- Continuous signals: advisories affecting vendor products (CISA KEV, CERT-In advisories), vendor status pages, news, security ratings.
- Performance against SLAs and security obligations; overdue remediation items.
- Access review of vendor accounts (Module 6).

When a vendor reports an incident, or one is reported about a product you use:

1. Determine exposure: which data, access and products are affected.
2. Invoke the contract notification and cooperation clauses.
3. Contain, by revoking vendor access and rotating shared credentials (Course 12 Module 6).
4. Assess your own notification duties (CERT-In, DPDP, customers).
5. Track the vendor's remediation.
6. Update the vendor's risk rating.
