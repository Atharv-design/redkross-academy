Concentration risk arises when many critical processes depend on one vendor or technology: one cloud provider, one endpoint agent on every device (the CrowdStrike outage, Module 5), or one identity provider. Manage it by:

- identifying shared dependencies in the BIA;
- designing for degraded operation;
- multi-region or multi-provider designs for critical services;
- pre-negotiated alternatives;
- staged update policies for agents and SaaS where vendors allow it.

Exit planning, for critical vendors:

- document how you would leave (data export, migration, timeline);
- test data export;
- keep contract rights to transition assistance;
- review the plan when the relationship changes.
