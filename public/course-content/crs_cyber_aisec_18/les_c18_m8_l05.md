1. Executive summary: what was assessed, overall risk, decision requested, top three actions.
2. Scope, authorisation and method (probes, runs, versions).
3. Failure-path register: FP-1 to FP-n with hops, evidence, controls, status.
4. Findings by OWASP category with counts and intervals (report.py).
5. Control evidence: unit tests, audits, provenance.
6. Recommendations with owners and dates.
7. Residual risk and limitations (small model, sample sizes, single-machine results).
8. Appendix: evidence file hashes, configuration, probe definitions.

Write facts and inferences separately and avoid unqualified words such as "secure" or "safe".
