Before adopting a model, record:

- publisher and repository; whether the publisher is verified;
- model card: intended use, training data description, evaluations, limitations;
- licence and acceptable-use terms;
- exact revision or digest (not "latest");
- file formats provided;
- your own evaluation results (Module 7) for your use case.

Prefer models from established publishers with documentation, and mirror approved models into an internal registry rather than pulling from public hubs at deploy time.
