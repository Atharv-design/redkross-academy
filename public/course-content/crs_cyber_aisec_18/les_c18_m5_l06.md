Models produce fluent but wrong statements (OWASP LLM09). When customers or staff rely on them, the organisation carries the consequence (Air Canada case). Controls:

- Ground answers in authoritative sources (RAG) and show sources.
- For policy, price and legal statements, answer from structured data or templates rather than free generation.
- State limits clearly and route to humans for consequential decisions.
- Test factual accuracy on a set of known questions (utility probes).
- Monitor complaints and corrections; fix the source, not just the answer.
