## Italy's Garante and ChatGPT, March-April 2023 ------------------------------------------------------------------- Context: Fortune, reporting with the Associated Press on 28 April 2023, stated that the previous month the Italian watchdog known as Garante had ordered OpenAI to temporarily stop processing Italian users' personal information while it investigated a possible data breach. ChatGPT became available again in Italy before an April 30 deadline after OpenAI added information on its website about how it collects and uses data used to train the algorithms behind ChatGPT, gave European Union users a new form to object to having their data used for training, and added a tool to verify users' ages at sign-up [C18-M04-R1]. Lessons: - AI processing of personal data is subject to data protection law like any other processing; regulators can and do act quickly. - Transparency about training data, rights to object and age assurance are concrete controls, not paperwork. - A security incident (Module 1 case) can trigger regulatory scrutiny of the whole service.
