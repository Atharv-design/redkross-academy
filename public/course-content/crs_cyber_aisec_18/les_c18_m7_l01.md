| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Is | authorised, goal-driven evaluation of a whole AI system (model, retrieval, tools, UI, monitoring) against failures that matter to its owner; creative and adversarially minded; evidence-based. |
| Is not | a benchmark score of a model in isolation; unauthorised testing of someone else's service; producing harmful content for its own sake. |

Scope includes security failures (OWASP LLM categories) and, depending on the system, other harms the owner cares about (accuracy, fairness, unsafe advice). This course focuses on security categories with benign probes. NIST AI 100-2 E2025 provides attack and mitigation terminology; NIST AI 600-1 discusses pre-deployment testing in its Generative AI Profile.
