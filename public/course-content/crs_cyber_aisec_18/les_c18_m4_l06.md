Obligations depend on jurisdiction; examples relevant to this library:

- India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: lawful purpose, notice and consent or legitimate uses, data minimisation, security safeguards, breach intimation, rights of data principals (Course 01 Module 7).
- The EU GDPR and the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of 13 June 2024) for organisations in scope [R3].
- Sector rules (financial, health, telecom) and contractual obligations.

**Practical steps:** document the purpose of each AI processing activity, the data categories, the lawful basis, retention, recipients (vendors) and safeguards; give users notice that they are interacting with an AI system and how their data is used; provide routes to object or delete where required. Legal interpretation needs qualified advice; this course flags the questions to ask.
