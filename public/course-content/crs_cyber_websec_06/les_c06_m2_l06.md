Scope is not paperwork you do once - it governs every action. Practical rules: enumerate only what is authorised; never follow a link or redirect out of scope; never test a third-party dependency's live service; and build tooling that enforces scope so a mistake cannot reach an unauthorised host. The lab tools model this: they refuse any host but 127.0.0.1/localhost in code, so even a typo cannot point them at someone else's system. In a real engagement, scope is defined in the rules of engagement (scope.md is the template), and acting outside it is both a contract breach and, potentially, an offence under the Information Technology Act, 2000.
