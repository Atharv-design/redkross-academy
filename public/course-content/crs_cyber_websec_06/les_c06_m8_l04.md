Remediation applies the fix; retesting proves it worked. A finding is NOT closed until a retest confirms the weakness is gone - and ideally that the fix did not break functionality or introduce a new issue. Retesting repeats the same safe verification used to find the weakness, against the fixed code, and records the result. The lab models this precisely: app_fixed.py applies every remediation, and retest.py runs the same checks against both the vulnerable and hardened apps side by side, showing each weakness as PRESENT on 8200 and absent on 8201. The deep dive shows the retest proving 10 of 10 closed - the evidence that the work achieved its purpose.
