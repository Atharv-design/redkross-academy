Authorisation fails for a few recurring reasons: (1) trusting the client - a hidden field, a cookie (role=admin), or a JWT claim the client can alter - instead of deciding server-side from the authenticated session; (2) checking authentication but not authorisation - confirming the user is logged in but not that they own the object or hold the role; (3) relying on obscurity - the endpoint is "hidden" but not protected; (4) inconsistent enforcement - the check exists on some endpoints but was forgotten on a new one. The through-line is the trust boundary from Module 1: access-control decisions must be made on the server from trustworthy state (the authenticated session), never from client-supplied data.
