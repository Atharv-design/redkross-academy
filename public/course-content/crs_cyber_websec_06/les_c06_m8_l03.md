A security report turns findings into action. Each finding should state: a clear title; the OWASP category and CWE; a CVSS vector, score and severity; the evidence (how it was confirmed, safely); the impact in business terms; and the remediation. Findings are ordered by risk so the client fixes the most important first. The report also needs an executive summary (how many findings, how severe, the headline risk) for decision-makers, and enough detail for developers to reproduce and fix. The lab's report.py produces exactly this from the confirmed findings: an executive summary and the ten findings in priority order with vector, evidence and fix. A good report is reproducible (the vectors and evidence let others verify) and actionable (every finding has a concrete fix).
