The capstone is a complete, authorised assessment of your own lab application, performed and evidenced end to end: write the authorisation and scope; map the application; verify each weakness with the minimum safe check; score with CVSS; produce a risk-ranked report with remediation; apply the fixes in a hardened build; and retest to prove closure. You submit the scope, the evidence (tool outputs), the report, and the retest showing the findings closed - the same artefacts a real engagement delivers. The mandatory gate is that everything was done only against your own authorised, loopback-only lab. Part C contains the full capstone brief and the assessment criteria.
