Design now for the day you respond (Course 08 Module 6; NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3, April 2025, which aligns incident response with CSF 2.0 [R6]):

- Out-of-band administration and communications: responders can meet, talk and reach consoles if e-mail, chat or the corporate IdP is unavailable or untrusted.
- Responder identities separate from production identity, with phishing-resistant MFA and pre-approved emergency roles.
- Immutable or offline backups, including identity configuration, with restore tested against the recovery time objective (RTO).
- Evidence readiness: logs centralised and retained, time-synchronised, with documented export and hashing (Course 09).
- Isolation capability: the ability to quarantine a segment, revoke sessions centrally and disable an identity quickly (Modules 2-4).
- Rebuild capability: infrastructure as code and golden images so that systems can be rebuilt clean rather than repaired in place.
