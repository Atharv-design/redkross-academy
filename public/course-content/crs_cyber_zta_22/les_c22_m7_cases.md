## Equifax, 2017 - controls that were not validated ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Context: GAO report GAO-18-559 (published 30 August 2018, publicly released 7 September 2018) describes the 2017 breach of Equifax's online dispute portal, which resulted in access to personal information of at least 145.5 million individuals. Equifax's investigation identified four factors: identification, detection, segmentation of access to databases, and data governance [C22-M07-R1]. GAO reports, according to Equifax officials, that: - Identification: the vulnerability notice was circulated using an out-of-date recipient list, and a scan a week after the vulnerability was identified did not detect it on the dispute portal. - Detection: a tool to inspect network traffic was installed, but an expired digital certificate - expired about 10 months before the breach - meant encrypted traffic was not being inspected. - Segmentation: databases were not isolated from each other. - Data governance: a database held unencrypted credentials for other databases. Assurance lessons: - "We have an inspection tool" is a claim; "inspected traffic was visible today" is evidence. Validate by effect, continuously (L04, L06). - Scan coverage must be reconciled with the asset inventory; a clean scan proves nothing about assets it did not see. - Ownership data (who receives notices) is part of the control and needs validation too. - Segmentation and data-governance requirements belong in the traceability matrix with tests (Modules 4-5).



## (FICTIONAL TEACHING SCENARIO): The green dashboard ------------------------------------------------------------------------- This scenario is fictional. Tamarind's compliance dashboard showed every zero trust control "in place" because each owner ticked a box. When the architecture team built a traceability matrix with tests by effect, three of twenty requirements failed: a legacy depot subnet bypassed the gateway, PDP decision logs were not reaching the SIEM, and one backup had never been restored. The dashboard was replaced by the matrix, and "in place" now means VALIDATED with evidence less than 30 days old.
