Two audiences, two documents:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Technical | reference architecture, views, standards, patterns, ADRs, the assurance matrix and the roadmap with dependencies. |
| Executive | one page: the risk being reduced (in business terms), the current and target maturity per pillar, the three phases with cost and benefit, the decisions needed now, and how progress will be reported (share of requirements VALIDATED). |

Good executive summaries ask for a specific decision ("approve phase 1 funding and the admin-MFA mandate"), state residual risk honestly and avoid tool names where outcomes will do.
