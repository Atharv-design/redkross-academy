## Change Healthcare, 2024 - a portal without MFA ---------------------------------------------------------------------- Context: In written and oral testimony to US congressional committees on 1 May 2024, UnitedHealth Group CEO Andrew Witty stated that on 12 February 2024 criminals used compromised credentials to access a Change Healthcare Citrix remote-access portal that did not have multi-factor authentication enabled. The attackers moved laterally, exfiltrated data, and on 21 February 2024 deployed ransomware attributed to the ALPHV group, encrypting systems; the company paid a ransom. Reporting noted company policy called for MFA on external-facing systems, but this portal did not have it turned on [C22-M02-R1]. Lessons: - Identity controls must apply to every internet-facing access path; policy is not the same as enforcement - verify continuously (Module 7). - Remote access portals are tier-0-adjacent: require phishing-resistant MFA and device trust, and log centrally. - Lateral movement after initial access shows why segmentation and privileged access tiering (Module 4, L06) also matter. ## (FICTIONAL TEACHING SCENARIO): The standing admin ------------------------------------------------------------------------ This scenario is fictional. A Tamarind engineer's everyday account held permanent cloud administrator rights. A malicious browser extension stole the session cookie; the attacker used the admin session to create access keys. With JIT elevation, separate admin identities and PAWs, the stolen everyday session would have carried no admin rights; with alerting on key creation for admin roles (Course 11 M05 detections) it would also have been caught quickly.
