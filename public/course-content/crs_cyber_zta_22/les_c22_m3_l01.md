**Zero trust is:** a set of principles and an architecture that removes location-based implicit trust and shrinks the "implicit trust zone" around each resource; continuous evaluation of identity, device and context; least privilege by default; assume breach.

**It is not:** a single product, the removal of all network controls, a one-time project, or "never trust anyone" applied so rigidly that work stops. Networks still matter (Module 4) - but as one signal and one enforcement layer among several, not the basis of trust.
