The CISA Zero Trust Maturity Model v2.0 (April 2023) has five pillars - Identity, Devices, Networks, Applications and Workloads, Data - and three cross-cutting capabilities - Visibility and Analytics, Automation and Orchestration, Governance - each progressing through Traditional, Initial, Advanced and Optimal stages [R2].

**Assessment method:**

- For each area, record the current stage with evidence (not opinion): test results, configuration exports, logs (Module 7 evidence ladder).
- Choose a target stage for the plan horizon from risk and business need; Optimal everywhere is rarely justified.
- Note blockers: legacy systems, contracts, skills, budget.

**Common honest findings:** identity ahead of devices; networks still flat at the edges; data classification incomplete; visibility present but not for PDP decisions; automation absent.
