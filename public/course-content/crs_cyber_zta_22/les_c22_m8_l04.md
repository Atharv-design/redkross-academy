**Each initiative states:**

- the pillar and stage it advances;
- the owner (a named role, not a team alias);
- its dependencies;
- the requirement ID(s) in the traceability matrix that will show it is done (Module 7);
- its exit criteria (for example "R8 VALIDATED with evidence < 30 days old").

This turns the roadmap into a measurable programme: progress is the movement of requirements from NO-EVIDENCE or FAILED to VALIDATED, not the number of tools bought.
