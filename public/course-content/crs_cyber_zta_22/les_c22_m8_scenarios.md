## Scenario 1

The board asks for "zero trust done" in six months.

**Task:** Respond.

> [!TIP] Model answer
> Explain zero trust as a multi-year programme; offer a six-month phase 1 with measurable outcomes (admin FIDO2, central decision logging, device inventory) and risk reduction expressed in business terms; report progress as requirements validated; set targets per pillar, not a single "done".
>
> **Lesson:** set expectations with phases and evidence.

## Scenario 2

Two business units want different PDP products.

**Task:** Decide using the reference architecture.

> [!TIP] Model answer
> The reference architecture defines the logical PDP role, the policy model, logging and interfaces; choose products that conform, preferring one policy model and decision log format; allow a second product only through an ADR with integration and evidence requirements and a review date.
>
> **Lesson:** architecture standards govern product choices.

## Scenario 3

Phase 1 is 80% done, but a new acquisition brings a flat network with 2,000 unmanaged devices.

**Task:** Adjust the roadmap.

> [!TIP] Model answer
> Finish phase 1 for the core; add an acquisition track: isolate the acquired network behind a PEP with no implicit trust, inventory devices, apply phase-1 identity controls to its administrators first, and plan its integration through the same phases; update maturity and the matrix.
>
> **Lesson:** roadmaps are living documents.
