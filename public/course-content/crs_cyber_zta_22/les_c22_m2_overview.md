## About this module

When applications run in many clouds and users work from anywhere, the network edge stops being a meaningful boundary. Identity - of people, devices and workloads - becomes the control plane: the place where access decisions are made and enforced. This module designs identity architecture: a central identity provider with federation, strong and phishing-resistant authentication, device trust as an access condition, workload identity, and privileged access that is separated, just-in-time and monitored.

## Why it matters

In February 2024, attackers used stolen credentials to access a Change Healthcare remote-access portal that did not have multi-factor authentication, according to the UnitedHealth Group CEO's testimony to the US Congress; ransomware followed nine days later (case study). One missing identity control on one internet-facing portal led to nationwide disruption.

## Where it is used

Workforce identity (SSO, MFA, conditional access), customer identity (CIAM), privileged access management, device management, cloud IAM (Course 11), service-to-service identity.

## Learning objectives

1. Design a central identity architecture with federation and lifecycle automation.
2. Choose authentication methods by assurance level, preferring phishing-resistant MFA.
3. Use device posture as an access condition.
4. Design workload identity without static secrets.
5. Architect privileged access: separation, just-in-time, PAWs, break-glass.
6. Reason about identity decisions with a policy model.
