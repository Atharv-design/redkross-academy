| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| IdP | central authentication and token issuance (OIDC, SAML); single sign-on; conditional access policies. |
| Federation | trust between IdPs and applications or partner organisations; restrict which external issuers and claims are accepted. |
| Lifecycle (JML) | joiner-mover-leaver driven by the HR system of record; automated provisioning (SCIM) and deprovisioning (the Course 17 M06 leaver finding is the failure mode). |
| Entitlements | role- or attribute-based groups; access requests with approval; periodic access reviews. |
| Customer identity | separate tenant/realm from workforce; bot and fraud defences; consent and DPDP notice integration. |

**Avoid identity sprawl:** each unmanaged local account is a bypass of central policy. Inventory service accounts and tie each to an owner.
