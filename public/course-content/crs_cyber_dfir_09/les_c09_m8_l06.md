**Quality checks before release:**

- Technical peer review. A second examiner checks key findings against the evidence, ideally reproducing them.
- Administrative review. Consistency, item references, hashes, spelling of names, dates and time zones.
- Version control. Draft labels, a change log, and controlled distribution.

Testimony. Expert witnesses explain their findings to a court. Prepare by knowing your report, your methods and their limits. Answer only what is asked, admit uncertainty, and stay within your expertise. In India, procedural requirements for electronic evidence (BSA Section 63 certificate; Module 1) and any appointment as an Examiner of Electronic Evidence (IT Act Section 79A) may apply. Take guidance from counsel. The capstone (Part C, C4) asks you to investigate a prepared case end to end and deliver a report using this module's structure.
