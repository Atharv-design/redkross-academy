The classic lifecycle in NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 2 (2012) has four phases: preparation; detection and analysis; containment, eradication and recovery; and post-incident activity. Rev. 3 (April 2025) replaces this with a CSF 2.0 Community Profile that places incident response inside organisation-wide risk management [R1]:

- Govern, Identify and Protect support preparation.
- Detect covers finding and analysing incidents.
- Respond covers containment, analysis and communication.
- Recover covers restoration.
- Improvement runs throughout.

The phase names remain the common working vocabulary, and this module uses them. Other frameworks, such as the SANS PICERL model (preparation, identification, containment, eradication, recovery, lessons learned), cover the same ground. The phases overlap in practice. Analysis continues during containment, and new findings can reopen scoping.
