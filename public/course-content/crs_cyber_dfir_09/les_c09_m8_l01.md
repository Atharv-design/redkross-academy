| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Executive summary | for leaders: what happened, impact, risk now, decisions needed. One page, no jargon. |
| Technical report | for IT and security teams: indicators, affected systems, root cause, remediation detail. |
| Forensic or expert report | for legal proceedings, regulators or HR: questions asked, evidence examined, methods, findings, limitations and the examiner's qualifications. Every statement is traceable to evidence. |
| Interim updates | during an incident, clearly labelled as preliminary. |

A single investigation may produce several of these. The facts must be consistent across them. Only depth and language change. Decide early, with legal counsel, whether a report is prepared under legal privilege, as this affects drafting and distribution.
