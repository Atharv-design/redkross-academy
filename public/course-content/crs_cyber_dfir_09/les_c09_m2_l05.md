At collection, photograph the device in place, record identifiers, and bag and seal it with a numbered seal. Hash the image, and record the hash on the custody form (Module 1 Practical P2) and in the case management system. For each transfer, record who, when, why and the seal check. For digital transfers, re-verify the hash on arrival. Storage needs access control, logging, environmental protection and redundancy. Keep at least two verified copies, one of them a "golden" master never used for analysis. Analysis is done only on a working copy, and the working copy is verified before each session. Steps 4-5 of the lab show why: once a working copy is modified, its hash no longer matches. That is acceptable for scratch work only if documented, and never for the master.
