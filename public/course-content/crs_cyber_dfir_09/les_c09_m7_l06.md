Hold a blameless review within about two weeks, while memories are fresh.

**Cover:**

- a timeline of the incident and of the response, including decision points;
- what worked, what did not, and why (process, tooling, people);
- root cause and contributing factors;
- metrics: time to detect, contain and recover; scope; cost and impact;
- actions with owners and dates, tracked to closure.

Feed results back into preparation: playbooks, detections, telemetry, architecture changes and training. Share sanitised lessons with peers or ISACs under appropriate TLP (Course 08 Module 5).
