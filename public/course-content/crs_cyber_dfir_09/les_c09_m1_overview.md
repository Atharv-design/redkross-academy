## About this module

Forensics answers questions about the past with evidence. What happened on this laptop between 14:00 and 15:00? Did this account copy the customer database? When did the attacker first log in? A forensic examiner answers such questions with evidence that someone else can check: the methods are documented, the original data is preserved, and every conclusion can be traced to its source. This module lays the foundations for the rest of the course: the principles that make work forensically sound, the places digital evidence lives, and the legal and ethical rules governing how it may be handled, with particular attention to India.

## Why it matters

Technical skill without forensic discipline produces findings that cannot be relied on. A wrongly handled image, an undocumented command on a live system or an overstated conclusion can wreck an investigation, harm an innocent person or make evidence inadmissible.

## Where it is used

Corporate incident response, internal investigations (fraud, misconduct), litigation support and e-discovery, law enforcement cyber cells, regulatory investigations and insurance claims.

## Learning objectives

1. State and apply core forensic principles.
2. Describe the forensic process (collection, examination, analysis, reporting).
3. Identify evidence sources across endpoints, networks, cloud and people.
4. Explain the Indian legal framework for electronic evidence, including BSA Section 63 and the Section 65B case law it succeeds.
5. Recognise ethical obligations: authority, privacy, proportionality, objectivity.
6. Plan an evidence collection strategy for a scenario.
