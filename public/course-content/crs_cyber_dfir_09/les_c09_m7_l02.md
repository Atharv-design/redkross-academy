| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Plan | IR policy and plan: roles (incident lead, technical lead, communications, legal), severity levels, declaration criteria, authority to isolate systems, and reporting duties (CERT-In 6 hours; DPDP once in force; sector regulators). |
| Playbooks | step-by-step guides for common incidents: ransomware, business email compromise, web server compromise, data theft, insider, cloud key exposure. |
| Contacts | internal, plus external: IR retainer, cyber insurer, legal counsel, CERT-In, law enforcement, key vendors. Keep an offline copy. |
| Tooling and access | EDR with isolation; memory and disk acquisition tools tested in advance (Modules 2 and 4); a forensic workstation; pre-approved cloud IAM roles for snapshotting (Module 2 L06); an out-of-band communication channel; and a "jump bag" with media, write blockers, cables and forms. |
| Telemetry | logs that answer IR questions: centralised, time-synchronised and retained long enough (Course 08 Module 2; AA24-038A showed dwell times of years). |
| Recovery foundations | offline or immutable backups, tested restores, gold images, and documented rebuild procedures. |
| Exercises | tabletop and technical exercises that test the plan with real people. Record the gaps found. |
