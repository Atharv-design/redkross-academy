## Ivanti Connect Secure exploitation, 2024 - appliances and integrity checks -------------------------------------------------------------------------- Context: On 29 February 2024, CISA, the FBI, MS-ISAC and partner agencies in Australia, the UK, Canada and New Zealand published advisory AA24-060B. It covered threat actors exploiting CVE-2023-46805 (authentication bypass), CVE-2024-21887 (command injection) and CVE-2024-21893 (server-side request forgery) in Ivanti Connect Secure and Policy Secure gateways. CISA reported that Ivanti's internal and previous external Integrity Checker Tool (ICT) failed to detect compromise. Actors cleaned up by overwriting files, time-stomping and re-mounting the runtime partition. CISA's lab research found that an actor may be able to keep root-level persistence despite factory resets. The advisory noted that memory and disk forensics were used in the analysis. It recommended collecting artefacts such as running processes and services, unusual authentications and recent network connections, while warning that collection on vendor-managed, encrypted appliances may be limited [C09-M04-R1]. Lessons: - Built-in integrity tools can be deceived. Independent forensics, including memory, may be necessary. - Plan in advance with vendors how to capture memory and disk from appliances. - Time-stomping and file overwriting (Module 3) appear in real intrusions. - Assume that a reset may not remove a sophisticated implant.



## (FICTIONAL TEACHING SCENARIO): The reboot that erased the evidence ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- This scenario is fictional. Tamarind's web01 showed odd outbound traffic, and an administrator rebooted it to "clear it". The implant was memory-resident, and after the reboot it was gone. So were the process, the connection and the decryption keys it held. The disk showed only a time-stomped launcher script. The investigation could not establish what data had been accessed. Lesson: capture memory before any action that ends processes.
