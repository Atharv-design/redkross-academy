## About this module

Course 08 Module 7 taught the SOC analyst's part in incident handling: escalating, communicating, recommending containment and preserving evidence. This module takes the incident responder's view of the whole lifecycle. It covers preparing before anything happens, confirming and scoping an incident, containing it without losing evidence or warning the attacker too early, eradicating every foothold, recovering safely, and learning from the event. Forensic findings from Modules 2-6 drive each decision.

## Why it matters

Recovery that skips eradication invites re-compromise. Eradication without scoping misses footholds. Scoping without preparation is slow. The difference between a contained incident and a business-wide outage is usually decided long before the incident, in preparation.

## Where it is used

In-house CSIRTs, managed IR retainers, cyber insurers' panel firms, national CERTs (CERT-In), and critical-infrastructure operators.

## Learning objectives

1. Describe IR lifecycle phases and how NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3 aligns IR with CSF 2.0.
2. Build preparation artefacts: plan, playbooks, contacts, tooling and access.
3. Confirm and scope incidents using forensic findings.
4. Choose containment strategies and time them correctly.
5. Plan eradication and validate it.
6. Recover safely, monitor for re-entry, and run lessons-learned reviews.

## Before you start

**Prerequisites:** C09-M01 to C09-M06; Course 08 Module 7.

**Estimated study time:** About 9 hours.
